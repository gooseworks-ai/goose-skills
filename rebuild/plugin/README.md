# GooseWorks

GooseWorks makes on-brand ads inside your AI app. It learns your brand from your website, reviews and files, then makes image ads and short video ads from what it learned.

## What you can ask

- "Set up my brand from our website"
- "Make three ads for our best seller"
- "Make a video ad from this review"
- "Make 12 videos for our teas"
- "Make videos from my sheet"
- "What should I make this week?"
- "Make 3 new hooks for my best ad"
- "Test new hooks on this video I uploaded"
- "Plan our holiday campaign"
- "Is this ad on brand?"
- "What are my competitors running?"
- "Pack these ads for Ads Manager"

## How it works

- GooseWorks only uses the claims, prices and proof your brand approves.
- Before anything is made, you see what it costs in credits and say yes.
- Changes to your brand are drafts. Nothing changes until you publish it.
- GooseWorks reads your Meta results and packs finished ads for you to put into Ads Manager. It never launches, pauses or changes ads on Meta. Connected accounts get daily reports and suggestions after a complete account-local day is available.
- Video ads are made on your computer by Goose Video, which sets itself up from one pasted line, with nothing to install first. Finished videos are kept safe in GooseWorks.

## Make variations

Pick one of your ads and GooseWorks makes variations of it that change only what you pick, like the hook. It can be an image ad GooseWorks made, a GooseWorks video, or a video from your computer or your Meta ads. When you bring in a video, you tick in the dashboard that you own it or have the rights to run it. You see one price for the whole test and say yes on the card or in the dashboard. GooseWorks packs the variations for you to put into Ads Manager. Once they run, it tells you which one beat the original.

## What's inside

- `skills/`: sixteen guides your AI app follows, one for each kind of job.
- `.mcp.json` and `mcp.json`: the address of the GooseWorks connector.
- `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` and `plugin.json`: the name cards for Claude and for ChatGPT, Codex and Cursor.
