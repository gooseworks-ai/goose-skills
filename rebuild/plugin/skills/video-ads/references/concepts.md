# Concepts

Every concept is a test.

## Shape

A concept is one buyer, one awareness stage, one angle, one hook, one format, one of the pack's offers or none, its proof, and one line on what it tests.

## Angle and hook

- Pick the angle from what the buyer already knows.
- Open on a hook of one of these kinds: a customer's own words, a specific moment, a contrarian take, a call-out to the buyer, or a true number from an approved claim. A greeting, the brand name or a vague question is never a hook.

## Evidence

Back each concept with evidence, strongest first:

1. this brand's results that Goose has judged
2. approved claims with their proof, and customers' own words
3. the person's goals and targets
4. competitor ads, as inspiration only

Name the evidence behind each concept, and the verdict that would show it worked. A concept with no evidence is a first guess: call it that.

## Sets

A set is 3 to 6 concepts that differ in buyer, angle or format. Each later round changes one thing.
