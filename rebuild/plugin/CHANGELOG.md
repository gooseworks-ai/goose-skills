# Changelog

## 2.3.1

- Competitor suggestions stay suggested; an explicit Watch activates the saved competitor and follows its automatically queued research.
- Software image ad variations do not require a physical product photo. Variable pricing and missing optional brand files do not block supported conceptual ads.
- Video references distinguish sustained use from measured performance. Finished videos and variations export for Ads Manager; Goose never changes Meta accounts.
- Meta guidance reads the server’s daily reports and suggestions, with account and date checks.

## 2.3.0

- **Ad variations**, a new guide, makes variations of one of your ads and tests them against it on Meta, with one price for the whole test. An image ad GooseWorks made can get a new hook, product, background, offer, CTA, ad copy or size. A GooseWorks video can get new text, spoken or visual hooks, a new voiceover, end card, ad copy or size. A video you bring in from your computer or your Meta ads can get a new text hook or hook clip, end card, ad copy or size, in 9:16, 4:5 or 1:1.
- You bring a video in on the upload page in GooseWorks, and only you tick that you own it or have the rights to run it. Your AI app never ticks it for you.
- A video test's price is the batch card, with one yes for the whole test. The variations are made on your computer like any batch of videos. A variation that didn't finish costs nothing, and is priced again in the same test. One you don't like is rejected, and goes into a new test.
- The start card has a fifth choice, Expand an ad, and you can ask for "3 new hooks for my best ad".
- Every guide now says a variation changes only what you picked, and everything else stays as your ad has it.
- Seven guides send variation work to Ad variations:
  - **What to make:** a Winning ad gets new hooks to iterate on it, and a Tired one gets 3 new hooks.
  - **Image ads:** one change to one ad stays an edit, and several variations to test go to Ad variations.
  - **Video ads:** new versions of a video you have go to Ad variations, and both guides write hooks the same way: the first 2 seconds, 6 words at most, each from a different angle.
  - **Meta ads:** reads a test's results, and packs a test's variations for Ads Manager, one pack per button.
  - **Reference videos:** new hooks on the exact video go to Ad variations, and a video only on Meta opens the upload page with it picked.
  - **Resize and animate:** new sizes inside a test go in the test's plan.
  - **Creative review:** looks at a variation next to the original and judges only what it changes.

## 2.2.0

- **Video ads** now covers one video or many at once, and the Video batches guide is folded into it. One video is a batch of one, with one yes to the batch's price.
- It plans videos from your own sheet, keeps your sheet's words as you wrote them and fills only the empty cells.
- Goose Video sets itself up on your computer from one pasted line, with nothing to install first. Your AI app runs only the setup or start command Goose gives it.
- After your sheet or your picks, it writes every row with the sizes you chose, then shows the price card. You can leave a row out there and undo it, and for one video pick its hook with See the script.
- It never answers a check where you listen to every track; it gives you the batch page for that one. A nearby check, the style's own look at the finished video, it answers itself after looking at and listening to every file, with a full review Goose checks before the video carries on.
- You keep or reject each finished video on the card, with a reason when you reject. A sample that didn't finish has Retry, and a batch that needs a new price has Get a new price.
- Fixes you pick on the card arrive as one message, and come back as one price that changes only those scenes.
- When a run ends, it says so once, in one line.
- Every guide tells Goose what you want made, with the products and style you named, so you skip choices you already made. When you mention the dashboard or your notes, it also lists the requests you saved in Goose for your AI app.

## 2.1.0

Eight new guides, sixteen in all:

- **Ad library:** find, approve, reject, tag, archive, delete or download many ads at once.
- **Ask the team:** put one question to you or a teammate in Needs you, and read the answer back later.
- **Brand files:** write and change notes, briefs and reports, upload files, sort them into folders, and share or download them.
- **Footage search:** find a moment in your own videos, or a brand photo, from your own words, and play it.
- **Reference videos:** find video ads worth learning from, read one scene by scene, and make a video that follows it. It also brings in a video you finished somewhere else.
- **Resize and animate:** make an ad in the other sizes Meta shows ads in, or turn a still ad or product photo into a 5-second moving ad.
- **Social inspiration:** find Instagram and TikTok posts on a topic, save the posts and ads you like with a note, and follow accounts for ideas.
- **Video batches:** plan several videos at once for one yes, share plans or finished videos with people who aren't signed in, and download every finished video together.

Changed guides:

- **Brand setup:** sets up a new brand in one step, offers the blog, social accounts and review pages it found on your site, and looks after every place Goose reads from.
- **Image ads:** plans each ad as a test, with who it's for, the angle, the hook, the format and the offer. It can follow the look of one ad you pick, turn a product photo into an ad, change just one part of an ad, and keep a finished ad's look as a team template. A new size now goes through Resize and animate.
- **Video ads:** writes three hooks, uses one real customer line word for word, backs every claim with an approved fact, writes the ad copy and picks a voice. It checks the frames before it calls a video ready, and can stop a video while it's being made.
- **Campaigns:** sets up a campaign's ideas with how many ads each gets, notes and starting references, then makes the ads from the ideas you approve.
- **What to make:** reads Goose's call on each ad, suggests three concepts with what backs each one, and saves an analysis as a report.
- **Creative review:** says whether an ad is safe to run and how likely it is to work, and approves, archives or brings back the product photos Goose made.
- **Competitor research:** adds what people say about your competitors, the competitors Goose suggests, and a deeper look at one.
- **Meta ads:** Goose only reads Meta for now. The guide reads deeper results, like each ad set, its setup and the ads worth more spend or a pause. It packs finished ads for you to put into Ads Manager, and writes new copy from what worked. It no longer launches, pauses or changes ads: you make those changes in Ads Manager.
