  List my top 10 Instagram and Facebook posts from the last 90 days by engagement and reach. Which

steps:
  - >-
    Connect your social accounts and Meta Ads read-only through a Claude connector or MCP server, or
    export post and ad reports and upload them.
  - >-
    Ask the playbook prompt for your top posts by engagement and reach, and which were never run as
    ads.
  - >-
    Check that a post's engagement is not mostly from existing fans or a giveaway. Engagement is not
    sales.
  - Run the best posts as ads on a capped budget with a stop date.
  - Compare them with current ads on cost per sale, and re-run the list monthly.

faq:
  - q: Is boosting a post the same as running it as an ad?
    a: >-
      Close, but running the post through Ads Manager gives you more control over audience, budget
      and tracking. Either way, judge it on cost per sale rather than likes.
  - q: Why might an organic post do well as an ad?
    a: >-
      It has already held attention with real people, without paid reach. That makes it a good
      candidate to test. Paid audiences differ from followers, so the result can change.
  - q: How much should I spend on the test?
    a: >-
      Set a cap you are fine losing, and a stop date. For example, a small daily budget for a week
      or two. Then compare cost per sale with your current ads before spending more.
  - q: Can I use another AI assistant for this?
    a: >-
      Yes. ChatGPT, Grok, Muse or any assistant that can read your post and ad performance can run
      this prompt, either through a connection or from exported reports.
