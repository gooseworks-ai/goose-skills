  For my store and these competitors [names], put side by side: price on comparable products,
  free-shipping threshold, return window, guarantee, payment options and the current offer. Show
  where I'm weaker and where I'm stronger.
then: Fix the gaps that are cheap to close. Lean into the ones where you're stronger.
steps:
  - >-
    Give Claude web browsing, or paste your competitors' shipping, returns and pricing pages into
    the chat. No login is needed.
  - Run the prompt with your store and the competitors you most often lose sales to.
  - >-
    Check that any higher price is explained on your page. Being pricier is fine if shoppers can see
    why.
  - Close the gaps that are cheap to fix, such as the return window or shipping threshold.
  - Make your strengths more visible on product and cart pages.
  - Re-run monthly.

cadence: Monthly
faq:
  - q: What should I compare besides price?
    a: >-
      Free-shipping threshold, return window, guarantee, payment options like express pay or pay
      later, and the current offer. Shoppers often decide on these when prices are close.
  - q: Is it bad to be more expensive than competitors?
    a: >-
      Not if your page explains why. Better materials, a longer guarantee or faster shipping can
      justify a higher price. The check is whether a shopper can actually see the reason on the
      page.
  - q: Can I use ChatGPT, Grok or Muse instead?
    a: >-
      Yes. Any assistant that can browse public store pages can build the same comparison from this
      prompt. If it cannot browse, paste each store's policy and product pages in.
  - q: Which gaps should I fix first?
    a: >-
      The cheap ones. Extending a return window or lowering a shipping threshold often costs less
      than a discount, and both are easy to test and roll back.
