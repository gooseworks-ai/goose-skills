faq:
  - q: Should I match a competitor's sale?
    a: >-
      Not by default. First watch whether your own conversion rate dips while their sale runs. If it
      holds, you may not need to react. If it drops, a different offer can work as well as matching.
  - q: Which competitors should I track?
    a: >-
      Your closest rivals: the stores selling similar products at similar prices to the same
      shoppers. A short list of three to five keeps a daily check quick and focused.
  - q: Can ChatGPT run this daily check?
    a: >-
      Yes. Any assistant that can browse the web, including ChatGPT, Grok and Muse, can run the same
      prompt. Some can also schedule it, or you can paste it in each morning yourself.
  - q: Is the Meta Ad Library free to use?
    a: >-
      Yes. It is public and shows the active ads for any page, along with when each ad started. No
      ad account or login is needed to read it.
