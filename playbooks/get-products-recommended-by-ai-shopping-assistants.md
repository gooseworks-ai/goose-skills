  When shoppers ask AI assistants for the best product, your brand may not appear. Using a web

  Ask: 'What's the best [product type] for [use case] under [price]?' and 5 similar questions. Which
  brands are named, and which sites are cited? Then read my top 10 product pages: do they clearly
  state use case, size, materials, price and who it's for?
then: Add those facts to your pages. Get onto the review sites and threads the answers cite.

  - Check the answers over a few weeks. They change day to day, so one run is not a trend.

faq:
  - q: How do AI assistants choose which products to recommend?
    a: >-
      They draw on pages and sources they can read, such as review sites, forums and product pages.
      Pages that state clear facts are easier to quote. The exact method varies by assistant.
  - q: What should my product pages say?
    a: >-
      State the plain facts a buyer asks about: what it is for, size, materials, price and who it
      suits. Put them in text, not only in images, so assistants and search engines can read them.
  - q: How do I know if it is working?
    a: >-
      Track the same set of questions every few weeks and record whether your brand appears. Answers
      change often, so look at the trend over time, not a single day.
  - q: Should I test this in ChatGPT too?
    a: >-
      Yes. Run the same questions in ChatGPT, Grok, Muse and other assistants, since each draws on
      different sources. The prompt works in any assistant that can browse the web.
