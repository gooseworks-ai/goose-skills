# Hypermotion

Fast product hero motion cut from one product clip, with spec cards between the shots. Routing rows are named as in the shot-routing guide.

## Shot list skeleton
1. 0-1.5 s intro card, one or two words.
2. 1.5-4 s crash zoom from wide to the product.
3. 4-5.5 s hero spec: the strongest brand-stated number.
4. 5.5-20.5 s five clip windows (orbit, slow-motion air effect, pull-back, stillness) between four 1.5 s spec cards.
5. 20.5-22 s CTA card.
6. 22-25 s the real logo lands and keeps moving.

## Routing rows
Hypermotion product · Kinetic type / motion card · Music · End card.

## Pacing
- 25 s and about 14 hard cuts; windows shrink from 2.5 s to 1.5 s.
- Every window is cut from one 15 s product clip. Music from the first second, about 120 BPM, drop near 20 s.
- Several products: about 6 s wide plus 4 s close each, 25-30 s for three.

## Nearest template
`product-hypermotion`

## Top 5 failures and fixes
- The product morphs mid-clip → one camera move per beat; end the prompt with locks: shape, label, one product, no hands.
- Shake on the product warps it → put the physics in the air around it (dust, shimmer, spray).
- Every clip turns macro → open the prompt with "product small, setting dominant".
- A studio end frame snaps the scene to blank → match the end frame to the setting, or trim before it.
- The end card freezes → keep held text moving slightly (about 1% scale, a few pixels of drift).
