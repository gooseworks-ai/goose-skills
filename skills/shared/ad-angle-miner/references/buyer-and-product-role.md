# Buyer case and supported product role

**Summary.** An angle needs a buyer who can recognize the situation and a supported
reason to consider this product. Generic problems and benefit labels are insufficient.
Research these before choosing a reference, then carry them into the script handoff.

## Extract a usable consumer case

Use the current campaign audience. Record the person's role or ordinary routine,
their specific task or decision, what they already have, their current approach,
the relevant constraint and what they want instead. Keep actual work objects:
product photos, an ad variation, a claim rule or a teammate's brief. A case may
express discovery or desire; do not manufacture pain for every product.

Attach source ids and scope limits. A forum author is not automatically a buyer,
a public post is not verified human speech, and a large agency's process is not
evidence about a small consumer brand. Mark an authored scenario as a hypothesis,
not a real customer, quote or result. Reuse relevant research; fetch a specific gap.

## Connect that case to the exact offering

State the supported path: what the buyer supplies or does, what the offering does,
the output or purpose, and why it matters to this case. For software this can be
assets → documented creative work → reviewable ads. For a consumer product it can
be a supported formulation/design role and its use, without inventing clinical
causality or personal efficacy. An ingredient name alone does not explain the role.

Record which facts support each part. Separate documented operation, advertiser
positioning and observed effect. Competitor complaints do not establish that this
product fixes the issue. Reject an unsupported promise before ranking it.

## Carry the message, adapt the execution

For new video banks, include `buyer_case` and `product_role` on each angle, as defined
in [video-handoff](video-handoff.md). Keep the stable angle id, promise and evidence
ids when adapting it to another recipe. Fit speakers, proof, visuals, timing and CTA
to the actual recipe. Do not copy a street encounter into a podcast or turn it into
a creator's invented purchase story. Essential footage gaps remain explicit.

The October 2026 human review found fuller street scripts much better after adding
concrete buyer tasks and useful explanations. That supports this direction for that
test; it does not prove cross-format quality or ad performance. Podcast and UGC
scripts still require their own human review. Model scores remain separate evidence.
