# Strategy — Fall subscription launch

## Goal
New monthly subscribers from people who have never ordered.

## Audience
Broad audience in the US, ages 25 to 54, existing subscribers excluded. Broad lets Meta find
buyers; the pixel already has purchase history to learn from.

## Objective and placements
Sales objective, optimising for purchases. Advantage+ placements, because the creative set
includes both 9:16 and 1:1 formats.

## Budget
$30 per day on one ad set for 14 days, inside the $60 daily ceiling in brand.md.

## Creative plan
3 ads running, picked by the owner from 9 generated (3x rule). Angles: freshness, ritual,
gift-ready packaging. Version 2 pauses the gift angle (see decisions.md).

## Test frame
Compare the three angles on cost per subscription after at least 7 complete days and 50
purchases in total, then keep the best two.

## Success metric
Cost per new subscriber at or below $45.

## Reasoning
A single broad ad set keeps the budget from splitting too thin at $30 per day. Angles differ
in message, not just colour, so the test can say something. Version 2 changed only the
creative mix because one angle was clearly behind.
