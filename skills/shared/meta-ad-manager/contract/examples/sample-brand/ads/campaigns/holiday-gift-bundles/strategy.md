# Strategy — Holiday gift bundles

## Goal
One-off gift bundle purchases between 2026-11-15 and 2026-12-20.

## Audience
Broad audience in the US plus past one-off buyers, who already trust the brand.

## Objective and placements
Sales objective, optimising for purchases. Advantage+ placements.

## Budget
Proposed $25 per day, rising to $40 per day in December. Awaiting owner approval.

## Creative plan
4 ads needed, so 12 will be generated. Angles: unboxing, "for the coffee snob", last-minute
shipping, bundle value.

## Test frame
Two ad sets (new vs past buyers) for the first 10 days, then shift budget to the better one.

## Success metric
Cost per bundle purchase at or below $20.

## Reasoning
Gift season rewards reach, so the budget ramps into December. Past buyers get a separate ad set
because they convert differently and should not hide the new-buyer numbers.
