# The first message (drafted here, sent by the human)

Load at step 7. Creator programmes stall at the first message: generic DMs get ignored, over-long ones read like a mass blast. This playbook drafts one per shortlisted pick; the human approves the names first, then edits and sends each message from their own account. Nothing is sent from here.

## Rules

- **Short.** DM: 60–90 words. Email: 90–140 words, with a subject line under 7 words.
- **Specific first line** — one real post of theirs, named ("your 'senior corgi on the stairs' video"), never "love your content".
- **One clear ask** — reply to a yes/no question ("open to trying it?"), not a call booking.
- **Say what's offered, honestly.** Gift version: what's in the box, no obligation to post. Paid version: "we'd like to pay for a post" and that rates and terms come next — **never a number in the first message** (the price range is the human's negotiating position, not an opener).
- **Claims**: only lines cleared by the claims gate; usually none are needed — talk about the creator's content, not product benefits.
- **No pressure tactics**, no fake scarcity, no "as a top creator in…" flattery.
- **Disclosure** is mentioned only in the brief, not the first message.
- **Channel**: the public business route found after approval (email or the platform's DM); if a manager or agency is listed, write to them, and say it's for the creator.
- **Placeholders** the human fills: `[your name]`, `[brand]`, `[LINK]` if they want to share one.

## Shapes

**Gift (seeding):**
> Hi <name> — your <specific post> is exactly how our customers talk about <problem>. I'm <your name> at <brand>; we make <product, one neutral line>. Could we send you <what's in the box>, no strings attached? If you like it and want to post, great; if not, no hard feelings. Want me to send it your way?

**Paid:**
> Hi <name> — <specific post> stuck with us: <one line on why it fits>. I'm <your name> at <brand> (<product, one neutral line>). We'd love to pay you for a <deliverable> around <angle, one line>, with your usual creative freedom. Are you open to a paid collab this <month>? If yes, I'll send a short brief and we can talk rates.

**UGC-for-ads:**
> Hi <name> — your <demo-style video> is the kind of content that works in ads: <one specific craft note>. I'm <your name> at <brand>. We're looking for <n> short videos to run as ads (paid per video, with usage terms agreed up front). Interested? I'll send the brief.

**B2B (LinkedIn voice, newsletter, podcast):**
> Hi <name> — your post on <topic> (<date>) got the right people talking: <one line on the commenters or audience>. I'm <your name> at <brand>; we help <who> with <problem, neutral>. Do you take sponsored posts / placements? If so, could you share your media kit and rates?

Each pick's message goes at the end of its `briefs/<handle>.md`, labelled "First message — draft, you send it".
