# Wave-1 success criteria

Load at step 6. A creator programme without a yardstick never learns: every creator "did fine". Before anyone is contacted, write down per role what a win is, when it is judged, and what happens next. The human reports results; the outcomes ledger compares them with these criteria and the next run reweights the roster (roles, tiers, price bands, vetting weights).

**Time the wave to the buyer's calendar, not to our launch.** Posts land when buyers are deciding: B2B tool buyers plan in January (Q1 budgets) and are heads-down through Black Friday to year-end, so a 1–10 December wave for ad-tool buyers is the worst window — move it to January. DTC gifting lands before the buying season (weeks before a holiday, not on it). Write the reason next to the dates in `wave-1.md`.

## Per role (approximate starting points — set them from the brand's own numbers when it has them)

| Role | Win metric | Read window | Renew / cut |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reach (paid or gifted)** | Post views ≥ the creator's organic median; saves + shares in their usual range; comments with purchase intent ("where do I get this?") | 7 days after posting | Renew if views ≥ median **and** intent comments appear; cut if under half the median or off-brief |
| **Seeding (gift-only)** | Post rate across the wave (expect ~20–40%); positive mentions | 21 days after delivery | Promote the posters with above-median views to paid; drop non-posters from the next wave |
| **Affiliate / hybrid** | Tracked orders on `[CODE]` / `[LINK]` vs the fee (first-order gross profit ≥ fee, or the walk-away logic in pricing §6) | 30 days | Renew if the fee paid back; renegotiate to more commission if close; cut if not |
| **UGC-for-ads** | When run as an ad: hook rate / thumb-stop and CPA vs the brand's target | 14 days of spend | Re-book creators whose asset beat the account's median CPA; cut those in the bottom third. **No ad history (new brand):** compare within the wave — hook rate and CPA against the wave's median, labelled "starting point" |
| **B2B voice / newsletter / podcast** | Clicks or demo requests on the tracked `[LINK]`; qualified replies; cost per demo vs target | 14 days (posts) · 30 days (newsletters, podcasts) | Renew if cost per demo ≤ target; cut if no qualified response. **No target yet:** a starting point the human confirms — e.g. at least 2 qualified demo requests or 5 qualified replies per placement — labelled "starting point" |

## What goes in `creators/<date>/wave-1.md`

- One block per role in the wave: the win metric with a number (from the brand's figures, or labelled "starting point"), the read window with dates, the renew / cut rule.
- The placeholders the human must create before posting: `[CODE]` and `[LINK]` per creator (a code and a tracked link each — the playbook never issues them).
- Who reports what, when: "After <date>, paste views / orders / replies per creator and I'll score the wave."
- The roster's `wave` column = `1`; `wave_result` is filled from the human's report (`win`, `partial`, `miss`, `no-data`).

## Read-back (next run, via the outcomes ledger)

Compare each pick's result with its criterion; say what the numbers support (which role and tier worked, whether price bands were right, which vetting signal predicted a win), with "directional only" under ~5 creators per group. Propose the changes; the human's yes before the plan or bands change.
