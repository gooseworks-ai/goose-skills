# Creators by company type

Load at step 1. Company type (from the brain) sets the platforms, the creator roles, what "gift" means, how fit is judged and how the brief is shaped. Do not force the DTC seeding playbook onto a B2B SaaS.

| Company type | Platforms | Roles that matter | "Gift" means | Fit judged on | Brief shape | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DTC beauty / skincare** | TikTok, IG Reels, YouTube | Reach micro-creators (seeding waves of 20–50), UGC-for-ads, a few mid-tier | Product (unit cost + shipping) | The skin concern the creator states in their own content; commenters describing the same concern | Routine / demo / "first impressions", honest texture shots | Cosmetic vs drug claims; no unsubstantiated before/after |
| **DTC pet** | TikTok, IG, YouTube | Reach micro, UGC | Product | Owns the animal (visible across posts); breed/age match | Day-in-the-life with the pet | No vet/medical claims unless signed off |
| **DTC food / beverage** | TikTok, IG, YouTube Shorts | Reach, recipe creators, UGC | Product | Cooks or eats the cuisine on camera; regional availability | Recipe / taste test / "what I eat" | Allergens, nutrition claims; alcohol age rules |
| **Consumer app** | TikTok, IG Reels, YouTube | Install-driving creators, UGC for app ads | Premium access, never cash-equivalent unless disclosed | Audience on the app's store region and language; content style that suits screen recordings | Screen recording + reaction; deep link `[LINK]`; Spark/partnership-ad rights; the creator uses the app before filming | Store-region mismatch kills installs; UGC-for-ads roles use the UGC rubric and per-asset pricing |
| **B2B SaaS** | LinkedIn, YouTube (educators), newsletters, podcasts, X | **The founder or cofounder first** (often the strongest creator the brand has: plan their posts with the organic plan), then B2B voices, educators, newsletter and podcast sponsorships. Before any paid pitch, a **free-access wave**: 20–30 practitioners who fit the buyer get a free account and no ask; those who post or reply become the warm list | A free account is **not** payment — paid or affiliate | Who engages: commenter headlines (function, seniority, company type); newsletter audience from media kits | Talking points + a demo flow; problem framing in the creator's voice; rights ask: Thought Leader ad permission on LinkedIn | Engagement pods on LinkedIn; ask for media kits where data is missing; price per placement; ask contract value and target cost per demo |
| **Local business** | IG, TikTok (geotags), local Facebook groups | Local micro-creators | A visit, meal, service | Share of the audience in the city, read from comments and geotags | In-venue visit post | Keep to the city; disclosure still applies to comped visits |
| **CPG in retail** | TikTok, IG, YouTube | Reach, recipe/use creators | Product or retail voucher | Lives where the product is stocked | Drive-to-retail ("find it at …") — no code tracking | Regional availability; seasonal timing |
| **Services / agency / consultant** | LinkedIn, podcasts, newsletters | Podcast guesting, co-hosted content, newsletter mentions | Rarely applies | Audience of buyers (headlines, sponsor lists) | Talking points | Creators rarely fit; often PR/podcast-guesting territory — say so and keep the list small |
| **Nonprofit** | IG, TikTok, YouTube (LIVE fundraising) | Cause-aligned creators, often unpaid | — | Cause alignment shown in their history | Story + call to give/act | Stricter brand-safety bar; charity-appeal and disclosure rules |
| **Marketplace** | TikTok, IG, YouTube | Both sides: supply recruiters (sellers/hosts) and demand creators | Credits on the platform | Which side the creator's audience is | One brief per side | Two different lists — don't mix |

If the brand sells in several modes (a SaaS with a consumer app, a DTC brand with wholesale), pick the mode the goal serves and say which.
