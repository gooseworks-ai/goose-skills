# Rough base rates by company type

Load at step 3. Use these to call a target unrealistic, to judge whether a number the founder gives is a problem, and to set the "worked if" line. They are wide, typical ranges from industry benchmarks and practitioner experience, not facts about this company: always say "roughly", never quote them as precise, and prefer the founder's own history whenever they have one. Categories, prices and channels move them a lot.

## DTC e-commerce

| Measure | Rough range | Read it as |
|---|---|---|
| Store conversion rate (orders ÷ sessions) | 1–3% (well under 1%: a page or offer problem; 3%+: strong) | Under 1% with traffic → conversion before more traffic |
| Repeat purchase within 6 months (consumables) | 20–40% | Under ~15% on a consumable → retention or product |
| Email share of store revenue, with flows running | roughly 20–30% (flows often half of that) | Under 10% with a list → flows are the cheap win |
| Welcome-flow conversion | a few % of new subscribers | — |
| Paid social return on ad spend at small scale | about 1.5–3× is common; below breakeven ROAS for the margin is the real bar | Falling with rising frequency → creative fatigue |
| Black Friday / Cyber Monday | often a fifth or more of Q4 revenue for gift-able products | Plan flows and offers 4–6 weeks out |

## B2B SaaS and sales-led B2B

| Measure | Rough range | Read it as |
|---|---|---|
| Cold email reply rate | roughly 1–5% typical; 5–10%+ with a sharp list and message | Under 1% → list or message, not volume |
| Reply → meeting | a third to a half of positive replies | — |
| Demo / discovery call → closed deal | 15–30% | Lower → fit or pricing, not top of funnel |
| Free trial → paid, no card up front (opt-in trial) | roughly 15–25% | Under ~10% → onboarding or fit |
| Free trial → paid, card up front (opt-out trial) | roughly 40–60% | — |
| Freemium sign-up → paid | roughly 2–5% | — |
| Design partners → paying customers | most of them, if fit is real; under half → fit first | Ask about weekly use before price |
| Design partners who keep using it weekly | most of them, if fit is real | Half drifting away → fit first |
| SEO / content payback | 6–12 months | Not a first move with weeks of runway |

## Consumer app

| Measure | Rough range | Read it as |
|---|---|---|
| Day-1 / day-7 / day-30 retention | roughly 25–40% / 10–20% / 3–8% (varies a lot by category) | Day-7 well under ~5% → product before marketing |
| App-store page conversion (views → installs) | roughly 20–40% | Low → listing first |
| Install → paid (subscription apps) | about 1–5% | — |
| Owned audience → waitlist | roughly 1–5% of followers join a waitlist when asked directly | — |
| Waitlist → launch-week installs | roughly 20–50% of an engaged waitlist | Plan the launch around the waitlist, not the follower count |

## Marketplace (two-sided)

| Measure | Rough range | Read it as |
|---|---|---|
| Fill rate (requests matched) | healthy above ~80–90% | Below ~70% → supply is the constraint |
| Time to match | the user's own patience window (hours for services, days for jobs) | Waits beyond it → supply |
| Repeat use by the demand side | high once trust exists | — |

## Marketplace seller (Amazon, Etsy, Walmart)

| Measure | Rough range | Read it as |
|---|---|---|
| Listing conversion (unit session percentage) | roughly 10–15% typical on Amazon; much lower on Etsy | Low → images, title, price, reviews before ads |
| Reviews needed before ads pay | a few dozen with a strong average | Under ~15 → reviews first |
| Ad cost of sales (ACoS) target | below the product's margin | — |

## Local business (single or multi-location)

| Measure | Rough range | Read it as |
|---|---|---|
| Google reviews vs nearby rivals | being within reach of the top three rivals' count matters more than the absolute number | Far behind → reviews first |
| Profile actions (calls, directions, site clicks) | grow with reviews, photos and complete hours | Flat after fixes → offer or location |
| New-customer offer redemption | low single-digit % of people reached | — |

## Services (agency, consultant, professional firm)

| Measure | Rough range | Read it as |
|---|---|---|
| Share of new clients from referrals | often half or more | No referral ask in place → that is the first move |
| Proposal win rate | 20–40% | Lower → positioning or fit |
| Sales cycle | weeks to months | Plan "worked if" in introductions and calls, not signed deals |

## Creator, newsletter and media

| Measure | Rough range | Read it as |
|---|---|---|
| Email open rate | unreliable since 2021 privacy changes; use clicks | — |
| Click rate | roughly 1–5% | — |
| Sponsor CPM | varies widely by niche | Ask, don't assume |

## Using them in "worked if"

- Pick a number the founder can see in their own tools within the stated time.
- Anchor on their own baseline when they have one ("repeat 1 in 6 → 1 in 4 in 60 days"), on these ranges only when they don't.
- Short horizons (2–6 weeks) for first moves; leading signals (replies, installs, reviews, flows live) before lagging ones (revenue).
