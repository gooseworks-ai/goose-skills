# run-organic-social: server needs

What this playbook needs that the GooseWorks MCP does not have today (staging, checked 2026-10-16). Each row gives the need, the step that needs it, and what the playbook does meanwhile.

| # | Need | Step | Meanwhile |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Posting, scheduling and a manual to-do queue that works for every platform, without a rendered creative.** Today `organic_creative_queue` takes only an approved *rendered* creative and only TikTok/IG destinations, and `social_post_update approve` hands posts to managed publishing. | Hand-over (the parked half of the spec, P3) | Drafts only. The human posts by hand from the posting sheet, or imports the CSV into their own scheduler. Neither tool is called. |
| 2 | **A calendar object in the brain**: dated slots with platform, account, pillar, bucket, status (planned / drafted / founder-approved / posted / skipped) and the final posted text. | Plan, hand-over, reconciliation; analyze-performance reads what went out | `social/calendar/<start-date>.md` in the brand coworker workspace. It is a working copy, not the brain. |
| 3 | **Account records for LinkedIn (personal and company page), X, Threads, YouTube, Pinterest and Facebook.** `social_account_upsert` and `social_account_list` cover TikTok and Instagram only. | Audit, voice cards, pillars | `social/accounts.md` in the workspace. Rules the human states are saved via the shared save-learning atom. |
| 4 | **Free own-post history and metrics for every platform.** Today `brand_read organic` gives about a week of own TikTok/IG posts for free; X (profile + `user-tweets`), longer Instagram/TikTok history and LinkedIn and Threads posts are paid proxy reads. `user-tweets` returns *popular* posts, not the newest. | Audit, voice cards, format-test read-back | Free read first; paid proxy read under the spend gate with the marketer's yes (check `created_at` on X); pasting as the fallback. |
| 5 | **Follower-activity times** (when this account's audience is online) from the platforms' own insights. | Posting times | The account's own best `posted_at` slots, or labelled starting guesses inside the human's free windows. |
| 6 | **A free trend feed per platform** (rising sounds, formats, topics) with first-seen dates. | Trend slots | Trends come only from this run's platform research, research-market's latest report ("What people are saying": trends with post-by dates) and the inspiration library. Flex slots carry an evergreen fallback, marked "trend: swap within 48h if one lands". |
| 7 | **Per-person voice store** in the brain. `brand_update` holds a single brand `voice` field. | Founder voice cards | `brand/voices/<handle>.md` in the workspace, confirmed by the person. Shared with make-content. |
| 8 | **Claim status on the server.** Research writes claims as `approved` with no human review, and no tool changes a claim's status. | Post check | Follows the claims gate's contract: the sign-off marker plus its "Claims you may use" must facts (proof in the Brand record). With no marker, plain product facts only. |
| 9 | **A seasonal and cultural calendar per region**, plus the brand's own dated moments (launches, promotions, do-not-promote windows) as structured fields. Section 19 of the knowledge map has no typed home. | Plan: fixed slots and silent days | `knowledge_search` for the brand's dates, and the human. Public holiday dates are looked up each run, never assumed from memory. |
| 10 | **A record of the human's edits to drafts** (draft text vs posted text) that the server can learn voice rules from. | Reconciliation | The human's edited text is kept in the calendar file. Repeated edits are proposed as `do`/`dont` facts. |
| 11 | **An approver field for posts in a named person's voice** (who may say yes for the founder or celebrity founder) in operating rules. | Person gate | Asked once, saved through the shared save-learning atom, and written on every PERSON YES NEEDED flag. |
| 12 | **Profile reads: bio, link in bio, pinned posts, follower count**, per account. | Audit: profile hygiene | Read the public profile in the host browser; otherwise asked in the draft-report message. |
| 13 | **Fuller own-post history for free.** `brand_read organic` returns about a week of recent posts, with some captions cut off. X history needs the paid profile + `user-tweets` read (see #4, #19). | Audit: cadence kept, best slots, voice-card corpus; **refill scoring** (a two-week refill can't see TikTok/IG posts older than a week, so they show as not read) | The playbook states the window it has, leaves cut-off captions out, and offers a proxy read (paid, under the spend gate) or a paste when the corpus is under ~8. |
| 14 | **Pending fact proposals.** `brand_update patch.facts` rows go live at once. | Voice rules inferred from edits | Asked as a question; written only on the human's yes, through the shared save-learning atom. |
| 15 | **A dated holiday and cultural calendar lookup** for MCP-only hosts with no web tool. | Seasonal pass | List the likely moments and ask the human to confirm dates. Never assume them. |
| 16 | **The free carousel renderer without a managed account, with PDF export.** `social_post_create` with `format: image_carousel` and `carousel.slides` renders hand-built slides free and deterministically, but only on a managed TikTok/IG account and only as a post draft. Needed: the same renderer for any brand (brand style from the kit), returning slide PNGs plus one PDF for LinkedIn document posts. Hosted chats have no shell to run goose-graphics. | Step 4: designed slots | Shell hosts render with goose-graphics and upload the files. Hosted chat hands over the slide copy (about 45 minutes by hand) and says so. |
| 17 | **One person's LinkedIn feed.** Only keyword search filtered by author exists, which gives a patchy sample for founder voice cards and the voices read. | Voice cards, refill voices read | Author-matched keyword search plus pasted posts; the patchiness is stated. |
| 18 | **Single-post reads and Bluesky in the verified endpoint list** (`/v1/twitter/tweet`, `/v1/instagram/post`, `/v1/linkedin/post`, `/v2/tiktok/video`, `/v1/youtube/video`; Bluesky search and profile). Owner: research-market (`../spend-gate/proxy-endpoints.md` (in the `spend-gate` skill)), after checking the vendor's OpenAPI. | "Why did this post do well?"; Bluesky niches | The host browser on the post page; Bluesky's public, keyless author-feed API. |
| 19 | **`user-tweets` newest-first, with a date window.** It returns popular posts, so "the last 30 posts" and cadence need `created_at` checks. Whether `since:` / `until:` / `from:` pass through X search is untested. | Own-account audit, voices read | Check `created_at`; test the operators in the first probe and note the result in the credits log. |
