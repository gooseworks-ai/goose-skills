# Benchmarks (honest ranges)

Load when judging an audit or a performance read. Ranges move; always say the source and its date when quoting one to the marketer, and judge a flow on revenue or conversion per recipient, never opens.

| Metric | Range | Source / date |
|---|---|---|
| Flow share of email revenue (stores) | ~30–50% healthy; Klaviyo reported flows ≈ 41% of email revenue from ≈ 5% of sends | Klaviyo benchmark, 2024-era data |
| Revenue per recipient, abandoned cart | ~$3.65 average; top 10% far higher | Klaviyo, ~2024 |
| Revenue per recipient, welcome | ~$2.65 average (third-party re-cuts ~$1–2.35) | Klaviyo, ~2024 |
| Revenue per recipient, campaigns | ~$0.10 | Klaviyo, ~2024 |
| Order rate, abandoned checkout | ~3–5% average | practitioner consensus |
| Order rate, welcome | ~2–3% average; 7–10%+ top tier | Klaviyo, ~2024 |
| Click rate, campaigns | ~1–2%; good > 2.5% | practitioner consensus |
| Click rate, welcome / cart flows | ~4–8% | practitioner consensus |
| Unsubscribe per send | < 0.3% fine; ≥ 0.5% fatigue or targeting | practitioner consensus |
| Spam complaints | < 0.1% target; 0.3% hard line | Gmail / Yahoo sender rules, 2024–25 |
| Hard bounce per send | < 0.5%; > 2% list-source problem | practitioner consensus |
| List decay | ~20–30% a year | practitioner consensus |
| SMS click | campaigns ~5–10%; flows ~10–20% | Attentive / Postscript / Klaviyo ranges, unverified primary |
| SMS opt-out per campaign | ~0.5–1.5%; > 2% too frequent | practitioner consensus |
| Push opt-in | Airship median ~49% iOS, ~53% Android | Airship, 2025–26 |
| Email share of store revenue | ~20–35% for mature DTC programmes | practitioner consensus |

Rule of thumb for order of value: checkout and cart ≫ welcome > post-purchase and browse > win-back. Under ~20% of email revenue from flows usually means flows are missing or broken.
