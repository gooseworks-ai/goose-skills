# Papertrail vs BillPilot — sales battlecard, 22 October 2026

## In short

**What we looked at:** the supplied product/pricing sheets, buyer interviews and three deal notes dated 19–22 October. All are fictional fixtures, not live market evidence.

1. **Lead with the review record for a team keeping its approvals** — first look from the small supplied deal sample.
2. **BillPilot fits a buyer needing native ERP write-back and payments** — confirmed within the supplied product capture; Papertrail does neither.

**So, do this first (because of 1 and 2):** the salesperson should ask whether the buyer needs a record before approval or a new payment system, on the next call.

## Decisions for you

Confirm that this deal's buyer is a controller keeping the current ERP and approvals. The supplied owner sign-off covers the exact product lines and price comparison below; broader claims remain unapproved.

## Deal fit

- **Lead with:** “Review invoice exceptions in a shared queue. Export the review history as CSV. Keep your existing approval steps.” These three lines are approved in the [supplied evidence](../../fixtures/focused-evidence.md), 22 October.
- **Where we may win:** controllers needing an accountable record while keeping approvals. Two supplied deal notes support this as a hypothesis, not a win-rate claim.
- **Where they fit better:** native ERP write-back and executing payments are essential. One supplied deal stayed with BillPilot for write-back. Do not promise a workaround we have not documented.
- **Where it is close:** both discuss invoice review. The available BillPilot capture puts history in Enterprise but leaves its export format and price unknown; do not claim we alone offer a review record.

## Evaluation questions

1. Which decision needs to be visible before your existing approval? If the problem is the review record, show that flow; if it is payment execution, explain our limit.
2. Does your process require native ERP write-back? If yes, Papertrail does not meet that requirement today.
3. What must the reviewer-history export contain? Test a real disputed invoice in both products before asserting parity.
4. How many reviewers need access? Compare the required tiers and seat minimums for that scenario, not just each headline price.
