## Brief starters

**Ratio update:** keep the existing page; explain the distinction between concentrate and ready-to-drink, identify which use the existing recipe supports, and link to serving/dilution. The article should point back to the main cold-brew guide.

**Dilution guide:** answer the serving task separately from how much coffee/water to brew. Use a tested example and clear instructions, with links to the ratio guide and relevant product only where useful. Exact recipe quantities and claims need their own sources before publication.
