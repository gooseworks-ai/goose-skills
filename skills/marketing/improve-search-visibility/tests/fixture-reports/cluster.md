# Cold brew coffee — topic cluster, 22 October 2026

## In short

This map serves a general reader learning to brew cold coffee at home. Use one broad cold-brew guide as the pillar and eight supporting pages for distinct decisions and tasks. Start with the pillar, ratio and dilution pages: brewing quantities and serving an already-made concentrate are connected but different jobs. This is a subject map, so no website, rivals, paid keyword numbers or publishing capacity is needed to complete it.

## Links and anchors

The pillar links to every supporting page with a clear task anchor, such as **choose your cold-brew ratio**, **dilute concentrate for serving**, **choose beans and grind**, **adjust steeping time**, **fix weak or bitter brew**, **store brewed coffee**, **make decaf cold brew**, and **compare iced coffee and cold brew**.

Every supporting page links back to the pillar using **cold-brew coffee guide**. Add useful sibling links in context: ratio ↔ dilution, ratio ↔ steeping time, beans/grind ↔ troubleshooting, steeping time ↔ troubleshooting, storage → dilution, and decaf → ratio plus beans/grind. Do not add every possible cross-link simply to inflate a link count.

## What to write first

Build the first three pages together so the beginner guide has useful destinations and the two quantity-related tasks stay separate. Then add preparation/extraction help, troubleshooting and storage. Decaf and method comparison expand coverage after the core path works. This is an order, not a calendar; no assumed production capacity is hidden in the dates.
