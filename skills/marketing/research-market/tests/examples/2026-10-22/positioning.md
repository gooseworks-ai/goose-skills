# Papertrail — positioning options, 22 October 2026

**So, do this first (because of 1 and 2):** the owner should choose which review problem to test with buyers before replacing the current positioning. The two choices below include finished safe messages.

## Evidence and current position

The product has a shared review queue, a recorded reviewer decision and a CSV review-history export. Human approvals remain. The exact approved lines are “Review invoice exceptions in a shared queue”, “Export the review history as CSV”, and “Keep your existing approval steps” ([supplied evidence](../../fixtures/positioning-evidence.md), owner sign-off 22 October).

The controller said, “I need to see who decided, not another score.” An AP lead said, “The next person asks me what happened, every time” ([supplied interview excerpts](../../fixtures/positioning-evidence.md), 19 October). These show a need, not proof of time saved. Two deals preferred keeping their approvals; another stayed with BillPilot for native write-back. This small sample cannot establish a win rate.

## Two strategic choices

| | Option 1: accountable review before approval | Option 2: shared exception handover |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer and situation | Controller reviewing disputed invoices across entities | AP lead passing unresolved invoices between staff |
| Plain category | Invoice-exception review tool | Shared invoice-exception queue |
| Alternative replaced | Email/spreadsheet notes lacking a connected review record | Unstructured staff-to-staff handover |
| Attribute → buyer value → proof | Recorded reviewer decision plus CSV history → a record to hand to a finance reviewer → supplied product sheet, 22 Oct | Shared queue → a place to continue the review → supplied product sheet, 22 Oct |
| Why choose it | Review record with existing human approval kept | Shared review workflow for the team |
| Where it loses | Buyer requires native ERP write-back/payment execution | Buyer already handles exceptions well in the existing tool; another queue adds little |
| Evidence strength | First look: three of five controller interviews asked for a record | First look: three AP interviews, one explicit handover quote |
| Unresolved proof | Whether buyers find the exported fields sufficient; no compliance or audit certification | Whether the queue improves handover; no measured time or error change |

Neither choice creates a new category or claims exclusive features. BillPilot's captured Enterprise history/export details are too thin to claim it lacks a record.

## Recommendation and counterargument

Test option 1 first. It links a specific buyer's decision to a documented output and fits the product's manual-review boundary. The strongest counterargument is that the controller's existing ERP already supplies a sufficient record. If buyers can retrieve the same decision history there, the supposed wedge weakens; compare the actual export fields before making a claim.

Option 2 is a real alternative buyer/problem priority, not just a warmer headline. Choose it if AP leads own the purchase and the handover example proves more important than the controller's review requirement.

## Finished draft messages — option 1

**Internal positioning statement:** For controllers who need a record of disputed-invoice review before approval, Papertrail is an invoice-exception review tool with a shared queue and CSV review history. It keeps the existing human approval step. The proposed alternative is loose email and spreadsheet notes; superiority over a specific tool is not established.

**One-line pitch:** Review invoice exceptions in a shared queue. Export the review history as CSV. Keep your existing approval steps.

**Homepage headline:** Review invoice exceptions in a shared queue.

**Subhead:** Export the review history as CSV. Keep your existing approval steps.

**CTA:** See the review queue.

**Boilerplate:** Papertrail is an invoice-exception review tool. Review invoice exceptions in a shared queue. Export the review history as CSV. Keep your existing approval steps. Papertrail does not pay invoices or write back into an ERP.

**For AP leads:** “Review invoice exceptions in a shared queue.” Pain evidence: the handover quote; product proof: the queue. Do not add “no more chasing” without evidence.

## Validation test

The owner shows the two message variants in alternating order to five suitable controllers/AP leads. Each buyer describes the product, names the actual exception they would use it for and checks a sample exported decision against their current workflow. **Pass for option 1:** at least four correctly describe review-before-existing-approval, and at least three identify a current decision-record task for which the sample fields are sufficient. **Fail:** they expect payment automation or cannot name a useful record requirement. These are proposed learning criteria, not industry benchmarks. The owner records answers and counterexamples; the next positioning run reads those actual results before strengthening the claim. No paid test is launched.
