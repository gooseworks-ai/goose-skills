# Disclosure — organic posts, email and creator content

Shared reference in the `ad-policy-check` skill, next to its SKILL.md (owner make-static-ads). Load it for content that is not a paid ad: organic social posts, newsletters and other email, creator briefs and creator posts, replies. Paid ads use `SKILL.md` in this folder. Craft knowledge, not legal advice; rules differ by market, so when one decides whether something ships, tell the human to confirm it.

## 1. Material connections (creators, affiliates, employees, gifted product)

- Anyone paid, gifted product, given a commission or discount code, or employed by the brand who talks about it must disclose the connection **clearly, in the post itself, before "more"**: "#ad", "Ad", "Paid partnership with <brand>", "Gifted by <brand>". Platform branded-content tools ("Paid partnership" label) are switched on as well, not instead.
- On video: said out loud and shown on screen near the start; on a story: on every frame that endorses.
- "#sp", "#collab", "#thanks", disclosure only in a bio or at the end of a long caption, or behind "more" — not enough (US FTC Endorsement Guides; UK CAP / ASA; EU UCPD; India ASCI).
- Creator briefs say what to disclose and how, and never script claims the brand could not make itself — a creator saying it is the brand saying it.
- Reviews and testimonials: real, verbatim, not edited to change meaning, typical results; no paid or incentivised review without saying so; never a review from someone who does not exist (US FTC rule on fake reviews and testimonials, 2024).

## 2. AI-generated or altered content

- Realistic generated people, voices or scenes that could pass as real: label them where the platform asks (Meta "AI info", TikTok AIGC, YouTube altered/synthetic content) and on the asset when a viewer could be misled.
- A generated person never presents as a real customer or employee.
- Synthetic-content transparency rules (e.g. the EU AI Act's) are evolving: one line to the human — "check disclosure needs for your market".

## 3. Organic posts

- Contests and giveaways: official rules linked, eligibility, no purchase necessary where required, the platform's own rules (no tagging in photos to enter on Meta, etc.).
- Claims in organic posts are claims — the same claims check applies.
- Health, finance, alcohol and other regulated categories keep their category rules in organic posts too.
- Reposting someone's content (UGC) needs their permission, and credit.

## 4. Email and SMS

- Commercial email: an honest subject line and sender, the brand's physical address, a working unsubscribe honoured promptly (US CAN-SPAM); in the EU/UK, consent before marketing email and SMS (GDPR / PECR), and a clear identity.
- SMS: explicit consent, opt-out words ("Reply STOP"), quiet hours where the market requires them (US TCPA and state rules).
- Offers in email follow the same rules as ads: real terms, a real end date, no fake scarcity.

## 5. Report

Per item: `pass` · `fixed (what, why)` · `flag (risk, rule, decision needed)`; "not checked: <rule>, <why>" when you could not check it.
