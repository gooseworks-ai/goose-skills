Rules:
- **The shape is write-report's**; its §3 checks block delivery (In short within 150 words, at most six items in What to do). The story opens with Checks and pacing (pacing against the ceiling with everything approved), then Since last time from the run-memory outcome check (`run-memory.md`), then This week, then Your top ads (the run record's table, plain names).
- **Decisions for you** holds the calls only the marketer can make: near-threshold kill-or-hold, graduation options, waiting targets and caps (§ The yardstick in the brain), unknown statuses and asks (once each, with the date asked). **What to do** holds the approval bundles (`meta-levers.md` § What "one-tap yes" really is today): the links, the pause sentence, the cards.
- Every move has its evidence and a check; holds have dates and trip-wires; near-threshold calls show both readings; collisions are shown and resolved; a pause never claims savings the budget doesn't produce.
- **How sure** in write-report's words — confirmed, likely, first look — never High / Medium / Low.
- **Every material fact links its dated source** by write-report §1 (an export links to the marketer's file): inline, in the moves table's Source column, or on the section's line; Sources lists them all. In short names its sources in words.
- **No ids, no tool or playbook names, no paths:** moves are numbered for this proposal only.
- **Keep these headings as written** (this playbook's steps and the scheduled prompt look them up): Since last time, This week, Your top ads, Decisions for you, What to do.

## Delivery (every run)

Deliver it through the `write-report` skill (`../../write-report/SKILL.md`) §2: topic `paid-moves`; brand copy `ads/campaigns/<slug>/proposals/<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`, title `Paid moves — <slug> — <Brand> — <YYYY-MM-DD>`, tags `[run-paid-campaigns, paid-moves]`, `keep-history`; local mode by its local branch. A scheduled run sends it to the operating plan's report destination when one is set. In chat, write-report's three lines: the first move with the findings it rests on, the link, and the decisions — the approval links and the pause sentence count as decisions.

**Verdicts on earlier moves** (the same words in the proposal, the run record and `decisions.md`'s Outcome line):
- **worked** / **didn't work** — extra results per day and what each extra result cost against the allowable, before → after, both windows named, labelled Meta's counts (claimed) or store-joined (truth), netted against the rest of the account.
- **too early** — with the date it becomes readable.
- **can't tell** — with the reason: another change on the same ad set in either window, a tracking break, stale data, no ad-set view (ads GooseWorks didn't make in the ad set and no export), or too few results to separate from noise. Say what would make the next one readable.
Never judge a raise on the new average cost per result, from unsettled days, or from a window shorter than its baseline.

## state.md — the server's campaign-harness contract

Path: `ads/campaigns/<slug>/state.md` in the brand's coworker workspace (slug from the brief's Status comment; else the campaign name, lower-case, hyphenated). The server finds the folder **only** through this frontmatter, closes recommendations **only** in the `## Open recommendations` section by deleting the `- R<n> (` bullet, and stamps `updated_at` / `updated_by`. It needs a coworker workspace — with none, server-side logging can't happen; say so.

Grammar (matched exactly):
- Frontmatter between `---` lines: `key: value` scalars and `[a, b]` lists, keys lower-case.
- `meta_push_ids: [<push id>, <push id>]` — every push id `prepare_meta_ad_push` returns is added here at once (the server matches outcomes by it), plus `goose_campaign_id: <id>`.
- `## Open recommendations` — one bullet per open move, on **one line**: `- R<n> (YYYY-MM-DD): <move> — evidence <…> — <status>`. When empty, the line `None.`
- Other sections are yours; the server leaves them alone. Keep every follow-up in `## Checks due` (one line per move: `- R<n> check YYYY-MM-DD: <rule> · trip-wire <…>`) — the server deletes a recommendation's bullet when it closes it, so a check written only in the bullet would be lost. ## decisions.md — the change log

Path: `ads/campaigns/<slug>/decisions.md`. **Append-only**, dates never earlier than the newest entry — an older event logged late takes today's date and names its real date in the Outcome line. The server appends a block for every Meta outcome it sees (approved, declined, paused, swapped) when the call named the push or campaign; this playbook appends, in the same grammar, what the server can't see: card moves once the marketer says "done", declines the marketer gives in chat, holds the marketer chose, and each check's result. Re-read immediately before appending (the server writes the same file).

Grammar (one line per field, no paragraph breaks):
```markdown
---
campaign: serum-launch
---

# Decisions — serum-launch

### 2026-10-13 — Founder story held
- Recommended: R10 — kill or hold (near threshold)
- Evidence: report:2026-10-13 — $410, 8 orders, CPA $51
- Decided: marketer chose hold; trip-wire $60 CPA at $800
- Outcome: open until 2026-10-20

### 2026-10-14 — Google budget lowered (applied by the marketer)
- Recommended: R17 — Search – Non-brand daily $80 → $65
- Evidence: user request — export 6–12 Oct, CPA $80 = 2× allowable, 14 conv / $1,120
- Decided: marketer said "done" at 09:12
- Outcome: applied; expected −$105/week; check 2026-10-20

### 2026-10-14 — Sales – Broad raise read
- Recommended: R5 — card — Sales – Broad daily $100 → $140 (applied by the marketer 2026-09-30)
- Evidence: user request — your export, pre 23–29 Sep vs post 4–10 Oct, 7d click / 1d view
- Decided: check rule from Checks due

```

After appending a card's or a hold's outcome, delete its `- R<n> (` bullet from `## Open recommendations` yourself (the server only closes moves made with `recommendation_ref`). **Launches and graduations** carry no `recommendation_ref` (`prepare_meta_ad_push` doesn't take one): once push status shows approved or declined, delete their bullet yourself and, if `decisions.md` has no block for that push, append one with `Evidence: push:<id>`. `Evidence` uses the server's forms: `change:<id>`, `push:<id>`, `report:<YYYY-MM-DD>` or `user request`.

Each **final** check result (worked / didn't work / can't tell) is appended as its own block (above) with the verdict word in the Outcome line, so the verdict lives in the log as well as in the run record. A `too early` appends nothing: it only rewrites the date on that move's `## Checks due` line, which stays until the final read.

Never keep a second ledger anywhere else; analyze-performance and plan-campaign read the `### YYYY-MM-DD — <title>` blocks to trace results to decisions. The run record (`run-memory.md`) links these two files and adds only the baseline recipe, the result numbers and the scorecard.

## The yardstick in the brain (targets and caps)

When `brain_*` tools are listed, the yardstick is also read from — and changes are proposed to — the Brand Brain, so every playbook uses the same numbers:
- **Read** at Before you start step 4: `gooseworks:brain_map` chapter 20 "Our plan" (`target_cpa`, `target_roas`, `decision.*`, `experiment.*`) and chapter 15 "How we work together", plus the operating plan (`brain_open` record type `operating_plan`): the daily spend cap and currency, protected campaigns and ad sets, approvers, and the emergency stop. **A confirmed emergency stop → stage pauses and fixes only; no launch, scale or swap.** Protected entities are off-limits like the brief's.
- **Precedence:** the approved brief → a **confirmed** brain target → `state.md` → derived or asked. When the brief and a confirmed brain target differ, say both and use the brief for this campaign.
- **Found or waiting items never steer the moves.** A target, cap or plan item that is found (an agent save) or waiting for the marketer's yes appears only under "Decisions for you" ("A $35 target is waiting for your yes in GooseWorks; I've kept using $40 from the plan"). It is never used to compute a verdict, a scale step, pacing or the ceiling, and never to stage a move, until it is confirmed. One cautious exception: a pending emergency stop, or a pending cap lower than the one in force, may **block** budget increases and launches this run (say so under "Decisions for you"); it never authorises a change — no pause, cut or swap is staged on its strength alone.
- **Propose, never set.** When the marketer states or changes the allowable cost, a target return or a cap in this conversation: the target goes through the `save-learning` skill (`../../save-learning/SKILL.md`)'s fact path (kind 20, `target_cpa` or `target_roas`, their words as the statement; `applies_to` the hero product when the campaign sells one product and the brand has other campaigns with other targets); a **daily** cap they state goes to the operating plan with `gooseworks:brain_save` `operating_plan` (`daily_spend_cap_minor_units`, `spend_cap_currency`) — a typed write this playbook may make directly because its spec owns those sections (AGENTS §8 "Two seams only", ruling of 20 Oct) — with source `you`, `is_public: false` and the marketer's words as `user_statement`. Every such save is **found, never confirmed**: plan, budget and target changes wait for the marketer's yes in the GooseWorks app, so say so in one line ("Saved; it waits for your yes in GooseWorks before it counts") and keep using the brief's number until it is confirmed. Never accept the proposal yourself. A monthly ceiling stays in the brief and `state.md` (the operating plan has no monthly money field; `server-needs.md`). Never from a scheduled run.
- Without `brain_*` tools: the brief, `state.md` and the offer to update the brief's Economics section, as before.
