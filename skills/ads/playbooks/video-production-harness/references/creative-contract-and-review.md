# Agent version

## Reference and creative contract

Before adapting an audiovisual reference, inspect its complete moving picture and
hear its complete sound at normal speed. Verify the reviewer's actual perception
capabilities: an extracted WAV, transcript or tool audio attachment does not prove
listening. Use a capable reviewer or the existing owner review surface for missing
responsibilities. Record sampled-frame, continuous-motion, isolated-speech and
mixed-audio coverage separately, with exact source identity, checksum, duration,
covered ranges and remaining gaps. Never call frames plus transcription a full watch.

Record each important reference beat beside the adaptation:

| Reference interval and observation | Adapted words | Required action/proof | Sound and speaker | Scene/route | Permitted change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Exact source interval; distinguish quotations from paraphrases | Locked line/copy | What must visibly happen, including outcome | Speech mode and sound cues | Stable scene and asset IDs | Explicit scope and reason |

Keep observed reference facts, brand facts and the user's adapted request distinct.
A source sauna test does not establish a different product's eight-person capacity.
Resolve a disputed reference mapping before using it as the comparison authority.
AI presenters may illustrate verified features, but must not impersonate actual
employees, owners or customers or invent firsthand results/testimony. Lack of owned
actor footage is not permission to strip every person/voice from the requested format.
Block missing essential beats and contradictory picture/direction fields; derive
both fields from the same scene contract. A diagram, logo or text card cannot
silently replace a requested live demonstration, routine or creator performance.
Put a feasible concrete alternative in the current script/ingredient review when
tools or assets cannot meet the original mechanism.

With a supplied reference, its agreed structure controls pacing and shot order;
templates supply compatible components. Without one, start from buyer, objective,
exact product, supported mechanism, offer and CTA. Reuse existing angle research:
first-party performance with context, owned ads, relevant category/competitor ads,
organic demonstrations, then reviewed internal concepts. Views or active-ad
duration do not prove conversion performance. Offer a few feasible concepts when
needed inside the current script review, not a new mandatory meeting.

The host computes creative_contract_revision from creative_contract, scenes, target
duration, aspect ratio and brand-context digest. Save that returned revision with
words, hook/payoff, required actions/proof,
product/variant, sound intent, permissible edits and required beat IDs. Material
changes invalidate affected approvals and review evidence, not unrelated valid
work. Estimated timings remain provisional until actual performances exist.

## Content determines review duties

Populate required_features from planned AND observed content, independent of the
genre label. A presenter tagged other still needs speaking-person checks. Relevant
host feature names include speaking_face, product, scale, hands, application, pour,
physical_demo and readable_text. Actual people, speech, sound and continuing worlds
still enable their applicable duties even when no enum names them.
Unexpected content adds duties rather than escaping them.

| Content | Required checks |
|---|---|
| People | Identity, anatomy, believable appearance/performance, wardrobe and relevant world |
| Speaking mouth | Full speech listening, voice/words/pronunciation, source alignment and perceptual lip-sync |
| Product | Exact variant, geometry, material, label, state and supported use; sourced scale where material |
| Action/proof | Complete entrance/action/exit, contact, state/order, object permanence and visible payoff |
| Text | Exact copy, caption-to-word timing, phone-size readability and obstruction |
| Sound | Intended cues, isolated speech where available, complete mix and clean joins/endings |
| Continuing world | Set/props, lighting/reflections/support and cross-shot geometry; deliberate changes remain valid |

Use [product scale and world](product-scale-and-world.md),
[speech and route qualification](speech-and-route-qualification.md) and
[libraries, look and rubric](libraries-look-and-rubric.md) for the applicable checks.

## Production eligibility and repair

Inspect every selected still before animation. For a new/unqualified creator route,
review the first actual face/voice/performance before ordering the remaining takes.
For difficult actions, prove a representative action with the same product and
conditions; a talking-head pass cannot qualify application, pouring or cooking.
An outstanding automated check is not a passed check. Parallelize independent
qualified work, preserving project/scene/revision identities. Each batch output needs
its own exact-source/final coverage and verdict; one passed example or an all-done
status cannot approve the remaining videos.

Inspect every complete generated clip. A failed clip or failed permitted redo is
ineligible for a passing assembly. Continue independent work, preserve the rejected
candidate and list the affected gap; never exceed the existing retry/budget bound.
Classify source/model, input, edit and perception-capability failures separately.
Repair a detached native edit locally instead of buying another correctly matched take.

Cosmetic crops/overlays are allowed only when they preserve the required product,
action, identity and proof. Do not hide changed packaging with a logo, wrong geometry
with a crop, or impossible action with a mask. Do not widen people. Never blindly
flip a mirrored take: verify writing, handedness, hardware and continuity first.
Use a faithful source, qualified repair or explicitly reviewed alternative.

## Exact-output review

Review the complete assembled export at normal speed with sound, including joins
and the full ending. Listen to isolated speech when available and the complete mix.
Add original-resolution, suitably dense motion/mouth inspection around suspicious
intervals. Two-fps contact sheets support visual findings; they cannot certify
continuous action, phonemes, pronunciation, voice character or music quality.

Record each applicable dimension as pass, needs_revision or incomplete, with exact
observed ranges and evidence. Keep technical file/probe/rebuild results separate
from content verdicts and owner decisions. A valid AAC stream, repeatable render or
matching transcript cannot certify natural speech, fine sync or absence of babble.
Corroborate ASR anomalies against actual audio; ASR can invent words in silence.
No overall score clears unusable speech, broken sync, wrong product, missing proof,
impossible action or unreadable essential text.

Bind reviews to output_sha256, creative_contract_revision, timeline_revision,
source/performance identities and reviewer capabilities/coverage. Any shot, sound,
timing, crop or caption change invalidates affected checks. Review every delivered
ratio/cutdown after its own final crop/text render. An older cut cannot clear a new
file. Preserve an owner's requested preview/final label separately from unresolved
quality; that decision does not cause or erase the production defect.

## Current GooseWorks review records

In quality_report.content_review, all dimension keys are present:
reference_and_concept, brand_and_product, scale_and_geometry, action_and_continuity,
lip_sync, voice_performance, pronunciation, sound_and_silence, pacing_and_joins,
camera_and_lighting and overlays. Each has status pass, needs_revision, incomplete

applicability rationale. The overall verdict is approved, needs_revision or incomplete.
These records describe actual review, not fabricated scores or helper completion.

Register each production clip's exact media_id and sha256 with its content_review:
reviewer, verdict, actual audio_perception/motion_perception, covered millisecond
ranges and findings. Existing five-category automated clip checks support this
record; they cannot establish full perception by themselves.

Reference observations carry exact media_id/media_revision, actual audio_perception
and motion_perception, and covered second ranges through the full source ending.
Frames/transcripts alone cannot assert those perception flags. Other bindings map
the same responsibilities to their real supported fields.

## Delegation and resume

Handoff the exact fetched skill/version/hash, reference observations, product facts,
creative contract, selected sources, current jobs/approval/budget, scene plan and
required duties. Require actual outputs/checksums, intervals, observations, rejected
candidates and remaining gaps in the return. A status sentence is insufficient.

Read authoritative saved state first. Reconcile job IDs and input fingerprints;
retrieve/poll existing jobs and reuse valid approved sources. Do not resend every
logged paid request. Retry only after confirming the endpoint's actual idempotency,
job state and unchanged authorization. A stale label or filename cannot authorize reuse.

The two mandatory owner gates remain script and ingredients. Integrate concept,
voice, anchor, look and sound choices into those existing surfaces. Agent checks at
still, clip and exact-final stages add no automatic approval meeting. Route missing
perception through an already available reviewer/owner surface and save the gap;
do not invent a new human sign-off to replace ordinary agent work.
