# Brand — Northwind Coffee (sample)

## Business model
Direct-to-consumer specialty coffee sold on its own Shopify store. Most revenue comes from a
monthly bean subscription; one-off bags and gift bundles make up the rest.

## Unit economics
- Average order value: $38 for one-off orders, $32 per month for subscriptions (per the owner, estimate).
- Gross margin: about 55% (per the owner).
- CAC target: $45 or less for a subscriber (per the owner, set at intake).
- Subscriber LTV: unknown — ask at strategy once Shopify is connected.
- Observed: 212 subscription orders in the window [window 2026-08-01..2026-08-31; synced 2026-09-20T14:05Z; source shopify:orders_summary]

## Sales cycle
Impulse to a few days. Most buyers land on a product page and check out in one visit; the
subscription page converts better after a first one-off order.

## Other channels
Instagram organic (main channel so far), a newsletter, and two local cafés.

- No health claims ("boosts energy", "healthier") and no named customers.
- "Roasted within 48 hours of shipping" is approved by the owner.

## Safety rules
- Never exceed $60 per day across all campaigns without asking.
- Exclude existing subscribers (Shopify customer list) from acquisition ad sets.
