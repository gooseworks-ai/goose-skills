# video-veo-3

## 1.0.0

- First version: Google Veo 3 / 3.1 image-to-video (veo3.1, veo3.1 fast, veo3, veo3 fast) on fal, in scenes mode (one clip per plan scene, timed to the voice or song spans) and images mode (one clip per still). Always silent (`generate_audio: false`), 4, 6 or 8 s clips, a per-scene seed, a still-shot word check, a total-length price guard, a keep window and a still fallback. Built on `_lib/scene-clips.mjs` (from video-kling 1.0.0).
