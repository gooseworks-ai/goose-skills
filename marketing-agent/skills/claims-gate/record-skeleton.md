# Brand record skeleton (for a first record created by a scoped sign-off)

Use only when no Brand record exists yet. Later scoped sign-offs add to it; a full brand pass would fill the rest (learn-my-brand, unreleased). Path `brand/records/<YYYY-MM-DD>-v1.md`, brand coworker scope; save through the `save-to-brain` skill (`../save-to-brain/SKILL.md`) (`versioned: supersede`, title `Brand record — <Brand> — <YYYY-MM-DD> v1`, tags `[learn-my-brand, brand-record]`: keep this first tag exactly, it is the record's fixed creator tag that save-to-brain supersedes by). The owner reads this file: no tool or playbook names in it. Proof and Permission are reopened before each use of a proof-required line (`claim-types.md` § Reopening proof); Permission is "n/a" unless the line names a customer, logo, result or backer.

```markdown
# Brand record — <Brand> — <YYYY-MM-DD> v1

Status: scoped sign-off only; full brand pass not done · Claims: <signed-off | pending-client> · Voice: provisional
Not legal advice.

## Claims you may use
| # | Wording (exact) | Allowed variants | Type | Scope | Qualifier | Proof | Permission | Review by | Approved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Decisions needed (the sign-off sheet)
| # | Wording | Our recommendation | Why | Proof on file | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Not approved (do not use)
| Wording | Why not | What would unlock it | Safe alternative |
|---|---|---|---|

## Unknowns
| Gap | Blocks | Assumption in use (guess) | Asked | Reopens when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full review of every claim the brand makes | wider copy | only the lines above are approved | not yet | when you set up your brand fully |
```
