# Claim types, risk and the proof each needs

Loaded by `SKILL.md` §1, §2, §3 and §4, and by `local-claims.md`. The full craft (with law and platform references) belongs to learn-my-brand, which is held back for now; this is the short version every job needs. Not legal advice.

**Proof required** (last column) marks the types whose proof must be on file before a line is approved, and reopened before each use (below). For them an OK is never enough: no proof, no line, whoever says yes. "Owner's word" marks business facts the owner may vouch for (`SKILL.md` §4.3).

| Type | Examples | Risk | Proof that makes it usable | Proof required |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product fact | ingredients, size, materials, integrations | low | the brand's own live product page, read in this job, or, on the brain path, a confirmed public record (`SKILL.md` §1.7) | no — a plain fact |
| Offer, price, policy | "free shipping over $35", "30-day returns", "20% off", "was $60" | low–medium | the live terms; an end date; a "was" price really charged; ads must match the landing page | no — live in this job (`SKILL.md` §1.8) |
| Efficacy / performance | "clears", "lasts 48h", "cuts reporting time in half" | high | a study or test measuring exactly this result on the finished product; "up to" only if many users reach it | **yes** |
| Clinical / expert | "clinically proven", "dermatologist-tested", "non-comedogenic", "hypoallergenic" | high | "proven": a controlled study; "tested": only with the result stated; "recommended": a survey of the experts; "developed": a named expert | **yes** |
| Consumer-perception stat | "94% saw smoother skin" | medium–high | the survey; say it was self-reported, with sample and duration | **yes** |
| Comparative / superiority / "the only" | "#1", "best", "better than X", "first" | high | a like-for-like test, or current ranking data with source and period | **yes** |
| Testimonial / review / named customer | a quote, a case study, a customer result, a logo | medium–high | the brand's confirmation that it is a real customer, the source and date, permission to use, any connection disclosed; must not imply typical results; a name matching another brand in the same account is a likely test brand, never offered before the human says it is a real customer that agreed to be named (SKILL.md §2.5) | **yes**, plus permission |
| Backer / investor | "backed by Y Combinator", "funded by …" | medium | a dated public source (press release, the investor's portfolio page) | **yes** |
| Rating or review count | "4.9 stars from 234 reviews" | medium | platform, count, date captured; recheck within 90 days | **yes** |
| Press quote / endorsement | "as seen in Allure" | medium | the article and date; the claim inside the quote passes for its own type | **yes** |
| Award | "6× award-winning" | medium | each award's body, year, category; stale after ~2 years unless dated | owner's word |
| Certification / attestation | B Corp, organic, cruelty-free, ISO 27001, SOC 2 report | medium–high | the certificate or report and its date; never "SOC 2 certified" or "HIPAA certified" | **yes** |
| Environmental | "eco-friendly", "carbon neutral", "recyclable" | high | specific certification; generic green claims are banned in the EU from 27 Sep 2026 | **yes** |
| Health / medical / safety | "treats acne", "reduces anxiety", "boosts immunity", "safe for kids" | high | depends on the regulator; for cosmetics and supplements usually never | **yes** |
| "Natural", "clean", "free-from", "non-toxic" | "100% free of …", "chemical-free" | high | a definition the brand can prove; "non-toxic" and "chemical-free": never | **yes** |

Puffery ("the comfiest tee you'll own") needs no proof but must not imply a measurable result. A product name is used verbatim as a name only, never stretched into a promise.

## Reopening proof before each use

A proof-required line is usable in a job only after its proof is reopened in that job, on every path:
- **Brain path:** `gooseworks:brain_open` the claim, then the evidence it points to.
- **Older path:** the line's row in the current Brand record (`SKILL.md` §1.3): its Proof and Permission cells.
- **Local mode:** `local-claims.md`, check 4.

Then check: the source still opens (a link loads, a file is readable); it still supports the **exact wording**, the scope (product, market) and **this channel**; it is inside its review date. A named customer, logo, result or backer also needs a permission on record that covers this use (older records with no Permission column: the permission must be named in the Proof cell). Anything missing, gone, narrower or unclear → the line is not usable (fail closed): cut it and say in one line what would bring it back ("I can't open the study behind 'clears acne in 7 days'. Send it again and I'll check the line again.").
