# Claim types, risk and the proof each needs

Loaded by `SKILL.md` §2 and §4. The full craft (with law and platform references) belongs to learn-my-brand, which is held back for now; this is the short version every job needs. Not legal advice.

| Type | Examples | Risk | Proof that makes it usable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product fact | ingredients, size, materials, integrations | low | the product record or label |
| Offer, price, policy | "free shipping over $35", "30-day returns", "20% off", "was $60" | low–medium | the live terms; an end date; a "was" price really charged; ads must match the landing page |
| Efficacy / performance | "clears", "lasts 48h", "cuts reporting time in half" | high | a study or test measuring exactly this result on the finished product; "up to" only if many users reach it |
| Clinical / expert | "clinically proven", "dermatologist-tested", "non-comedogenic", "hypoallergenic" | high | "proven": a controlled study; "tested": only with the result stated; "recommended": a survey of the experts; "developed": a named expert |
| Consumer-perception stat | "94% saw smoother skin" | medium–high | the survey; say it was self-reported, with sample and duration |
| Comparative / superiority / "the only" | "#1", "better than X", "first" | high | a like-for-like test, or current ranking data with source and period |
| Testimonial / review / named customer | a quote, a case study, a logo | medium–high | the brand's confirmation that it is a real customer, the source and date, permission to use, any connection disclosed; must not imply typical results; a name matching another brand in the same account is a likely test brand, never offered before the human says it is a real customer that agreed to be named (SKILL.md §2.5) |
| Backer / investor | "backed by Y Combinator", "funded by …" | medium | a dated public source (press release, the investor's portfolio page) |
| Rating or review count | "4.9 stars from 234 reviews" | medium | platform, count, date captured; recheck within 90 days |
| Press quote / endorsement | "as seen in Allure" | medium | the article and date; the claim inside the quote passes for its own type |
| Award | "6× award-winning" | medium | each award's body, year, category; stale after ~2 years unless dated |
| Certification / attestation | B Corp, organic, cruelty-free, ISO 27001, SOC 2 report | medium–high | the certificate or report and its date; never "SOC 2 certified" or "HIPAA certified" |
| Environmental | "eco-friendly", "carbon neutral", "recyclable" | high | specific certification; generic green claims are banned in the EU from 27 Sep 2026 |
| Health / medical | "treats acne", "reduces anxiety", "boosts immunity" | high | depends on the regulator; for cosmetics and supplements usually never |
| "Natural", "clean", "free-from", "non-toxic" | "100% free of …", "chemical-free" | high | a definition the brand can prove; "non-toxic" and "chemical-free": never |

Puffery ("the comfiest tee you'll own") needs no proof but must not imply a measurable result. A product name is used verbatim as a name only, never stretched into a promise.
