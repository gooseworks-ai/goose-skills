# server-needs — work-with-creators

What this playbook needs that the GooseWorks MCP does not have (checked against the staging tool list on 2026-10-16). For each: the need, the step that needs it, and what the playbook does meanwhile. Ordered by priority.

| # | Need | Step | Meanwhile |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A creator roster store** in the product: creators per brand with vetting snapshot, price range, deal recommendation, brief, and a status the human updates (shortlisted → contacted → quoted → signed → posted → results), visible in the app, readable by plan-campaign and analyze-performance | 8, roster-outcomes | A living `creators/roster.csv` in the brand coworker workspace (the §8 convention), kept by the shared outcomes ledger, plus a dated roster document imported into brand knowledge. Server-side, but unstructured and invisible in the app; a brand without a coworker falls back to the caller's own workspace. |
| 2 | **A free price check for public-data lookups** (the shared spend-gate's probe stands in) — `data_price_provider` covers fal and ElevenLabs only; `quote_only` exists only on `data_post_provider`. The server rule is "the customer's yes to the credit total first". | Gates 1–2 | The human sets a **ceiling in credits** at each gate; the playbook shows lookup counts by kind and the charges earlier answers actually reported, never its own multiplied total, and stops before the next lookup could pass the ceiling. On a first-ever run, one lookup is spent before any price is known. |
| 3 | **Rate data** — benchmark CPMs and flat rates by platform, tier, niche and geography, refreshed; and a place for real quoted rates across brands (anonymised) | 5 | Dated benchmark bands in `references/pricing-and-deals.md`, cross-checked with the brand's own quoted rates and creators' public media kits. Ranges, never single numbers. |
| 4 | **Audience demographics beyond TikTok countries** (IG and YouTube countries, age bands) and **audience authenticity** (follower-quality scores, growth history) | 4 | Fit from what commenters say about themselves plus the creator's content, with a confidence level; authenticity from observable signals, labelled "signals, not verified". |
| 5 | **LinkedIn post engagement with commenter headlines** | 4 (B2B branch) | Uses whatever the post data exposes; if commenter headlines are missing, says "commenter mix not checked" and lowers confidence. |
| 6 | **Newsletter and podcast data** (subscriber counts, downloads, sponsor rates) | 2, 4, 5 | Public advertise pages and media kits found by search; candidates marked "size creator-claimed — request the media kit"; conditional price ranges. |
| 7 | **Scheduled runs with an enforced credit ceiling** — `automation_upsert` agent jobs run the coworker (which uses credits) and have no spend-limit field, so the per-run ceiling lives only in the job's prompt | 9 | Per the shared spend-gate automation rule: offered opt-in, created paused, the yes names a standing per-run data ceiling and that the run itself uses credits. Each pick also carries `revet_by`, flagged free on the next run. |
| 8 | **Partnership-ad lookup by creator** (which brands ran partnership ads with this handle) | 4 | Searches competitors' ad-library pages for the handle; misses non-competitor deals. |
| 9 | **Follower-growth history** (spikes = bought followers) | 4 | Not checked; replaced by view-rate, uniformity and comment-quality signals. |
| 10 | **A free cache read** of public-data answers already fetched for this brand | 4, roster-outcomes | Reuses its own workspace notes (verdict numbers and dates); a re-vet pulls only the last 30 days. |
| 11 | **A reference-only tag in the Inspiration library** — a way to save creator posts as "reference, no usage rights" that ad and content playbooks will not remix | write-back | Creator posts are not saved to Inspiration; links live in the roster and briefs. |
| 12 | **Document import cost** — `knowledge_import` says a source "may spend credits"; an uploaded document has no limit field | write-back | Treated as free; confirm. |
| 13 | **Watching a creator's video** (frames or a summary of any public post, not only saved inspiration) | 4 (UGC rubric) | Delivery marked "not watched"; 2–3 links for the human to watch before approving names. |
| 14 | **Sending / ops layer**: outreach and DMs, gifting and shipping, discount codes and tracked links, redemption tracking, content approval, payouts | — | Out of scope by decision (D4). The human does all of it from the roster; analyze-performance keeps creators as a named future row. |
