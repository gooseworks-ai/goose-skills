# concept-grid

**Read:** the request, bounded source coverage and evidence in run.json; `../make-static-ads/references/workflow.md` is the record contract. **Write:** candidate reasons, selected ad rows and selection review in that same record.

1. Gather reasons in source order: approved campaign, angle bank, winners/rotation, customer language, usable product/brand facts, marketer's words, observed own ads/social topics, category research, labelled kit fallback. Missing customer bank → inspect public product reviews as insight/hook sources. A hypothesis may propose a scene; proof of its purchase reason remains separate.
2. Draft at least twice the selected reason count from at least three actual source types. Each names the persona/source and proof/source/status, plus why that proof supports this reason. Compare against checked own ads/history; preserve partial coverage. Missing grounded reasons → stop for research, the user's reasons or a smaller justified set. Avoid inventing reasons to meet a quota.
3. Score the five gate checks with reasons: specific supported promise, usable proof/demonstration, phone-size hook, three-second message and persona fit. Fix or reject any failure. Keep the rejected candidates and why. `creative-taxonomy.md` is optional craft/examples when a gate or diversity decision is unclear.
4. Develop each selected ad from an exact product fact and a specific human tension: what the person wants, what gets in the way, and why this product belongs in that moment. Research may ground the situation; an authored situation stays a labelled hypothesis. Turn that tension into a relationship and a visible event, rather than merely putting the feature in use. A demonstration can be the idea when watching the action resolves the buying question.
5. For every fresh/more concept, compare at least three materially different visual meanings. Develop each into a credible contender: a clear event, product relevance and a reason a buyer would care. Repair a contender that loses only because of an avoidable claim, packaging or staging problem before comparing it; an unusable spectacle is a rejected draft, not a substantive alternative. Camera/caption/palette variants are one premise. Choose on immediate understanding, distinctive idea, brand specificity and feasibility together; “safest” or “clearest” alone does not settle the choice. Explain what the winner adds beyond showing the product's ordinary function, or why a direct demonstration best meets this brief's proof need. Only an explicit request to reproduce a supplied scene or demonstrate a specific action may constrain fresh work to one option; quote it. Neither surrealism nor unusual props is required.
6. Shape the set around distinct buying decisions and visible ideas, not just different labels for product use. Cover the words and name each picture's event: repeated hand/product/flow scenes may still communicate one idea despite different food, crop or framework. Develop or merge overlaps before compose. Preserve valid demonstrations where they serve a specific need; an ordinary fresh batch still needs authored ideas that earn attention through this brand's customer situation. Match the researched personas and brief’s awareness; use live offers only. Name unfillable gaps rather than padding.
7. Give a fresh critic the request, actual cited source records, official product images, viewed references and alternatives. Ask it to challenge both **validity** (truth, rights, proof/persona fit and feasibility) and **creative strength**: are the contenders competitive, does the selected event make the human tension visible, what would be lost if a similar product replaced ours, and does the whole set offer meaningfully different ideas? Brand specificity can come from a product role and customer behavior together; an interchangeable logo or unusual prop is not enough. When a reference contributes a relationship, check that it survives in the selected event. Return findings against specific choices, including valid but generic winners or weak comparisons; “all options differ and are allowed” is insufficient. Resolve those findings before a pass. Without delegation, obtain a real human critique with its source, or mark selection blocked; same-agent review cannot clear it. The upstream checker in `../make-static-ads/scripts/README.md` checks recorded work, not the quality of these decisions.

Iterate/rotation retains the parent's supported reason/proof and changes one named lever. An exhausted reason requires the fresh path. Record the approved parent/request as basis. No new concept tournament for a rejected-element fix or copy-only change.
