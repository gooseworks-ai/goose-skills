# make-content: server needs

What this playbook needs that the GooseWorks MCP does not have today (staging, checked 2026-10-16). Each line gives the need, the step that needs it, and what the playbook does meanwhile.

| # | Need | Step | Meanwhile |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A content library in the brand brain.** Briefs, drafts, repurposed sets and published pieces as typed records (format, status draft/approved/published, URL, source asset, claims-check result), searchable by `knowledge_search`. | Output and write-back; brief step (avoid duplicates, find internal links) | Workspace files under `content/` in the brand coworker scope, plus `knowledge_import url` for pieces the human says are published. The files are working copies, not the brain. |
| 2 | **Per-person voice store.** A founder's or author's voice card, separate from the brand kit voice (`brand_update` exposes only one `voice` field). | Bylined pieces; shared with run-organic-social | `brand/voices/<person>.md` in the workspace. The person confirms it in chat. |
| 3 | **Story bank and opinions store** (section 10 of the knowledge map: the founder's opinions, bets, story bank, topics on and off limits). | POV interview, so answers are never asked twice | `content/pov/*.md` workspace files. Rules the human states go to `brand_update patch.facts` as corrections. |
| 4 | **Claim proposals.** A way to submit a new claim (customer name, number, quote) to the brand's claims sign-off. Claims are not writable through `brand_update`. | Claims check; case studies | Cut claims are listed in the handover with the proof needed. The human approves them through the claims gate's scoped sign-off in a later job (or confirms them in the GooseWorks app when the Brand Brain is on). |
| 5 | **Claim status on the server.** Research writes claims as `approved` with no human review, and no tool can change a claim's status. The claims gate works around this with a sign-off marker plus a Brand record document. | Claims check | Follows the claims gate's contract: the marker plus its "Claims you may use" must facts. With no marker, the scoped sign-off for the lines the piece needs; declined or unanswered lines → plain product facts only, and the rest is listed as "needs your sign-off". |
| 6 | **Transcribe a public video or podcast URL** (YouTube, Vimeo, a podcast RSS item). `media_upload url` records a URL without fetching it, and `fetch` takes images only. | Repurpose: getting the source in | Ask for the file (upload + `media_analyze`), the transcript, or pasted captions. |
| 7 | **AI-answer and rising-question data.** Which questions are rising in search and in AI answers for a topic. The proxy reads organic Google page one (`/v1/google/search`), but has no AI-answer, People-Also-Ask or volume data. | Brief | Top results via the host's web search or one proxy read under the spend gate; questions from the page brief, the voice-of-customer bank and the marketer. AI-answer coverage is stated as "not read". |
| 8 | **The brand's own long-form channels**: blog posts and their traffic, the newsletter archive with open and click rates. (LinkedIn and Threads posts are read through the proxy, author-matched; X posts are pasted; TikTok and Instagram come from `brand_read organic`.) | Brief (what performed); refresh (decay reason) | Ask the marketer for URLs or the decay reason; analyze-performance or improve-search-visibility supply the "why refresh". |
| 9 | **Fetch a public web page as text** for MCP-only chat hosts with no web tool (read a competitor page, a source article). `knowledge_import url` ingests into the brain but does not return the text to the agent. | Repurpose source in; comparison facts | Shell hosts use their own fetch. Chat hosts ask the human to paste. |
| 10 | **A saved channel list per brand** covering every platform (X, LinkedIn, YouTube, newsletter, blog, Threads, Pinterest), not just TikTok/IG managed accounts, plus a house-style field. | Repurpose: which platforms; every draft | Asked once and saved to `content/channels.md` in the workspace. Not saved as brand learnings, because the ad playbooks read those as creative rules. |
| 11 | **Pending fact proposals.** `brand_update patch.facts` rows go live at once; only kit and knowledge fields have a pending state. | Write-back of inferred voice rules | An inferred rule is shown to the human and written only on their yes, as their correction. |
| 12 | **Upload a large recording from a chat host with no shell.** `media_upload` takes `file` (a presigned PUT, which needs a shell or HTTP client) or `bytes` (≤~8 MB). | Repurpose: a webinar or podcast source | Ask for the platform's transcript or caption export, or for the file to be uploaded in the GooseWorks app; then `media_list` + `media_analyze`. |
| 13 | **Rights metadata for third-party IP and real people** in the product catalog: licensed characters, creators and celebrities, with what the brand may say. | Claims check: the third-party IP row | Generic wording unless the human confirms a licence or permission. |
