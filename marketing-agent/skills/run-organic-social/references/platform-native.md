# Platform rules a good draft still gets wrong

Load at the drafting step, **after** this run's platform research (the `run-organic-social-platform-research` skill, `../../run-organic-social-platform-research/SKILL.md`). This is a floor of stable mechanics; where the dated research says otherwise, the research wins. Rewrite every post for each platform: a shared core idea is fine, shared text is not. This file keeps only the mechanics that change reach or get a post flagged, which a capable writer still misses. Items marked (?) may have shifted; treat them as soft defaults.

## Per platform

**X**
- Links: X's then head of product denied a link penalty (Apr 2026; re-check in the research), while third-party data still shows link posts doing worse. Report both from the research and pick a hedge, usually the link in the first reply.
- Premium: X lists subscribers as a factor in reply ranking. For any reply-led plan, research its current effect and price and make a recommendation.
- Use 0–1 hashtags.
- A single strong post usually beats a thread. Never open a thread with "🧵" or "A thread:".

**LinkedIn, personal profile**
- Only the first ~140–210 characters show before "…see more", so the hook must land there.
- Put links in the first comment or drop them (?).
- Document (PDF) carousels earn above-average dwell time.

**LinkedIn, company page**
- Reach is a few percent of followers. The page works as a credibility archive and a thing employees reshare.
- Write it as a confident changelog in the "we" voice, not founder-style opinion posts.

**Instagram**
- The strongest signal for reaching non-followers is **sends per reach** (DM shares). End carousels with a "send this to…" prompt, not "like this".
- **Slide 2 of a carousel needs its own hook,** because people who skipped are re-served the carousel at slide 2.
- Captions are searchable, so keywords go in them.
- Use ≤5 hashtags (?).
- Posts carrying another app's watermark lose reach.
- Use the 3:4 or 4:5 frame.

**TikTok**
- Business accounts may use only the **Commercial Music Library.** Never script a trending song for a brand account; a trending format with original audio is fine.
- Say the search phrase in the spoken line, put it in the on-screen text and in the caption.

**YouTube Shorts**
- There are no clickable links. Point to long-form with the related-video link.
- The title is a search phrase.
- It is a good home for TikTok re-cuts without the watermark.

**Threads**
- Posts run to ≤500 characters, with one topic tag.
- Real questions work; bait doesn't.

**Facebook**
- Page reach is low, and Reels carry what reach there is. It is often the primary platform for local businesses and older audiences.
- Groups are out of v1, except announcement copy for the brand's *own* group.

**Pinterest** (visual and consumer brands only)
- It is a search engine, not a feed. Use 2:3 pins with a text overlay that promises a searchable how-to. The outbound link is the point.
- Pins live for months, so plan seasonal pins 45–90 days ahead and never post trends.

**Reddit and communities**
- Out of v1 posting. Self-promotion without a participation history gets removed and can start a backlash.

## Every draft

- No hashtag tails.
- No engagement bait ("comment YES", "tag 3 friends") unless the brand already does it and the platform allows it. A comment vote that genuinely decides the next post (choose-your-path, A or B) is content, not bait.
- Alt text on every image post.
- Keep on-screen text out of the bottom ~20% and the right edge (UI safe zones).
- The voice card's casing, emoji set and punctuation habits win over any norm here.
