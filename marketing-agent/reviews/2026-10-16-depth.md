# Depth critique — 17 marketing-agent playbooks (2026-10-16)

**In short**
- **The craft is senior-grade almost everywhere.** No playbook would produce output a senior would throw away when the brand's claims are signed off. Average depth ~4.1 / 5. The strongest are run-paid-campaigns, analyze-performance, email-lifecycle, make-product-photos and learn-my-brand.
- **Two things hold the set back, and both are cross-set.** (1) The claims gate. With no sign-off on file, which is the normal first run, every maker falls back to "product facts only" and offers a playbook switch. That gives ads and scripts with no benefit line, which a senior would rewrite (**MAJOR**). (2) SKILL.md bloat. Each file runs 1,700–3,600 words, mostly tool protocol and state rules added during the critique rounds, sitting inside the process steps.
- **Several research outputs stop at the inventory and miss the decision.** research-market doesn't rank its segments. improve-search-visibility has no cluster-to-page roadmap. plan-campaign buries its call under 16 sections.

Panel: performance marketer, lifecycle/CRM lead, content & SEO lead, brand strategist, social lead, B2B demand-gen lead. Severity: **MAJOR** = a senior would rewrite the output; **MINOR** = they would edit it. Points already fixed or deliberately rejected in each `critique.md` are not repeated, except where noted.

## Per-playbook

### 1. where-to-start — 4
**Run (two-person candle brand, $6k/month, wants TikTok ads):** It reads the lean brain, asks 4 questions and names the constraint: retention, because 1 in 6 buy again and no flows exist on a 900-person list. It recommends email-lifecycle (welcome and post-purchase), puts TikTok ads under "not yet" (a $500 budget can't exit learning, and new traffic would pour into a leak), notes Black Friday six weeks out, and sets "worked if: repeat 1 in 6 → 1 in 4 in 60 days". A senior advisor would stand behind it.
- MINOR: "Use no other data: no web research". A senior glances at the homepage, pricing, store and ad library before advising. Without that glance, a thin-brain run asks Q1 cold and can't catch the founder's misread of their own funnel.
- MINOR: the base rates are four bullets, so the numbers in "worked if" are guessed per run.
- Guide-ish: most of the value is the playbook map plus discipline (≤5 asks, one constraint, ≤2 picks). The map belongs in the foundation router (already noted in its critique).
- **Fix:** a bounded free glance before the questions, plus a per-type base-rate table.

### 2. learn-my-brand — 4.5
**Run (Personal Day, acne-safe skincare):** The output:
- a claims table with proof, scope and review dates (free shipping $35, product facts, "for acne-prone skin");
- the risky value props rejected or reworded ("100% free of acne-triggering ingredients" becomes "formulated without <list>"; "6× award-winning" waits on the award list);
- a never-say list tagged law/platform/owner (drug claims, Meta personal attributes);
- a 4-rule voice guide with real do/don't pairs and an unknowns ledger, ending with a fresh-read probe.

A compliance-trained brand strategist would sign it.
- MINOR: SKILL.md is 3.6k words. Step 7 is a full protocol (save order, marker wording, record versioning) that belongs in the atom. The model has to carry it through every step.
- MINOR: "who it's for" is a one-liner with no ranked reasons-to-buy. That is by design (write-my-positioning is backlog), but downstream makers inherit claims and voice without a message hierarchy.
- **Fix:** move the save protocol into `brain-write-back`.

### 3. research-market — 4
**Run (Personal Day):**
- ~45 kept quotes, from Reddit, TikTok comments, on-site reviews by fetch and labelled rival reviews.
- Themes such as "everything breaks me out" and "is it fungal-acne safe?" (a pre-purchase question).
- 3 segments plus an anti-persona ("expects prescription results").
- A demand read that is mostly proxy and prior, the recognition test, and an audience proposal.

The quote integrity, JTBD forces and stratified sampling are senior-grade.
- MINOR: the segments come out unranked. plan-campaign needs "which segment first and why" (share of evidence, pain intensity, WTP signal, reachability, product fit). Without it, the founder picks by gut.
- MINOR: there is no message implication per segment (what to lead with, which objection to pre-empt). The positioning playbook is backlog, so nothing translates the findings into messaging.
- **Fix:** add a segment-priority table and a primary-segment call, with a two-line message implication each.

### 4. research-competitors — 4
**Run (Personal Day vs CLEARSTEM, Paula's Choice, The Ordinary):**
- Three files with dated H1s, offer architecture and funnel.
- Ad clusters from the server sample, such as CLEARSTEM's "ingredient checker" (6 variants, ~90 days).
- A reasons-to-buy matrix: "ingredient transparency" contested, "fungal-acne safe" a gap if the voice-of-customer bank supports it.
- An angle bank A-01…A-08. Without a sign-off, most angles are "needs proof".

The ad-reading honesty rules are excellent.
- MINOR: the answer to its headline question ("what's working for them") is capped by a ~24-ad sample whose selection is unknown. The playbook rightly forbids "stopped" and counts without the full library, so the core answer stays thin unless someone opts into the full read.
- MINOR: the 14-step teardown per rival is heavy; content, social and releases rarely change the angle bank on a first run.
- **Fix:** make the full ACTIVE library read the default for the 3 deep rivals.

### 5. listen-social — 4
**Run (Personal Day, week of 12 Oct):**
- A coverage table, "≥14 brand mentions (cap hit Q2)" and sentiment counts with n.
- A theme "shipping delays to Canada" (n=4), and one S2: a 40k creator posting about "purging breakouts". It gets an on-post reply, a private draft and an escalation; the holding statement is held.
- 2 outliers saved (5× median "ingredient check" format), and a peaking "shelfie" sound with a post-by date.

A social lead would ship it.
- MINOR: SKILL.md is 3k words. Step 4 (counting window, `in_counts`, re-run merge, baseline after skipped weeks) is state machinery, not process. The critique rounds 3–7 fixed it inline.
- MINOR: for a brand with a dozen mentions, the full report shape is overbuilt. The quiet-week line exists, but the template doesn't scale down.
- **Fix:** move the state rules into an atom, and add a short-form report below ~20 items.

### 6. research-audience — 4
**Run (restaurant-scheduling SaaS, 50 GMs and owners at 10–50 location groups):**
- The fit definition played back, then a 5-account sample with real charges.
- 38 people at 22 groups. Email status is mostly `pattern-guess` or `none`, labelled honestly.
- Signals such as "hiring a scheduling coordinator, posted 2026-10-02", one hook per row, region flags, and a funnel summary.

A demand-gen lead would email ~80% of it.
- MINOR: tiers use fit × timing only. ABM tiering also weighs account value: a 50-location group deserves 1:1 depth that a 10-location one does not.
- MINOR: "why we're writing" is one line per segment. A senior hands outbound a 3-line angle (pain, proof, ask), so the human's sequence doesn't start blank.
- D2: the timing multipliers (×⅔, ×⅓) are close to a rulebook. They are defensible for re-tuning from outcomes, and the playbook says "adjust with judgment".
- **Fix:** add an account-value dimension and a per-segment angle.

### 7. plan-campaign — 4
**Run (Personal Day Black Friday push, $8k, "600 new customers by 2 Dec"):**
- An economics box: AOV ~$42, contribution ~$22, allowable CAC ~$26.
- The baseline subtracted. Implied CAC breaks the goal, so a renegotiation case comes back with levers.
- Channel rows: Meta Run, email Run, Search Test, TikTok Later, CTV No (economics)…
- T1 an angle test with readable thresholds, a backwards calendar with claims sign-off by T-30, a 12-static + 3-video asset list, and UTMs.

The math is senior; so is the "zero paid is valid" stance.
- MINOR: the founder has to dig the decision out of 16 sections, including retail-media and CTV rows for a 2-person brand. The "five lines in chat" helps, but the saved brief has no one-page decision.
- MINOR: the intake is long: a common list of ~11 items plus 5–6 per type. Most items become labelled assumptions, which is right, but the first message is heavy.
- **Fix:** one-page decision summary first; collapse irrelevant mandatory rows.

### 8. make-static-ads — 4 (3 for an unsigned brand)
**Run (Personal Day, Meta, no sign-off on file):**
- The state note, then a brief asking to approve 3–5 lines in learn-my-brand or go facts-only. The founder picks facts-only.
- 6 ads: pain hooks in buyer words ("11am and my face already feels tight"), product facts, the shipping offer, varied archetypes.
- Every benefit line ("non-greasy finish", "won't break you out") cut with restore-proof.
- Quotes, renders reviewed letter by letter, and a batch file.

With a sign-off, the worked example is senior-grade.
- **MAJOR (unsigned):** an ad with a pain hook and no benefit is a set a performance creative would rewrite. The gate is right; the route out (a playbook switch, then re-read) loses the session.
- MINOR: "fewer than three awareness stages fails the set" is a rule for judgment. A cold prospecting set should lean problem- and solution-aware, and the rule pushes unaware or most-aware ads into it.
- MINOR: "Engine facts" (≈300 words of `ads_generate` mechanics) sit in SKILL.md, not in a reference.
- **Fix:** an inline, scoped sign-off for the load-bearing lines through a shared claims-audit atom (see cross-set #1).

### 9. make-video-ads — 4 (3 unsigned)
**Run (Personal Day mist, Reels):**
- One 30s 9:16 ready-made style with a situation-POV hook and the brand narrating (no generated customer).
- The real product image on label shots and a claim trace.
- A review note ("the automatic read shows X at 0:21 — please confirm"), then 3 launch-copy options and a launch-notes file.

Script craft and honest review are senior-grade.
- MINOR: one video is not a test. The earlier critique rejected hooks up front on cost, and that reasoning is sound. But offering the hook test only after the watch-through adds a second spend cycle, when the human could decide once at the gate with both prices in front of them.
- MAJOR (unsigned): same as statics.
- **Fix:** a two-option quote at the script gate.

### 10. make-product-photos — 4.5
**Run (32 oz insulated bottle, colourway "Sage"):**
- A shot list from the store page: hero, back, scale-in-hand, lid detail, gym and desk lifestyle.
- References curated (two store images flagged as possibly AI by filename) and a house-style card confirmed.
- The hero first, then a fidelity check: 4 keep, 2 reject (`extra_or_missing_part`: two loops; `finish_wrong`: chrome highlights). Then a page order.

An e-commerce creative director would ship it.
- MINOR: in a chat host the agent can't see pixels, so "check every output against the real product" becomes "not checked by eye" plus a generic checklist. The structured `media_analyze` read could carry a field-level diff against the reference read (part counts, label text, colour words) instead.
- **Fix:** field-level diff in cannot-see mode.

### 11. run-paid-campaigns — 4.5
**Run (DTC, $9k/month Meta + Google):**
- A header: allowable $40, high-volume regime, pacing vs ceiling, 31% coverage, blended aMER below break-even.
- Last week scored. A graduate-or-not choice (A/B/C), a pause with no saving claimed, a near-threshold "your call" with both readings and a trip-wire, a rotate, a stock-out pause, and a Google card.
- Links, a pause sentence and cards.

Breakdown effect, marginal cost, Poisson noise and "no scale while blended is below break-even" are senior performance thinking.
- MINOR: there is no first-run baseline of the account itself: how many ad sets sit under the learning floor, spend concentration by concept, creative age. Seniors start every new account there, and it would seed consolidation and rotation moves from day one.
- **Fix:** a first-run structure and creative-mix audit.

### 12. analyze-performance — 4.5
**Run (same DTC):**
- Plumbing ok (overclaim 1.26×), a 3-line headline, a business-truth table (MER, aMER, nCAC, contribution vs break-even), and two stories side by side with the cheapest settling test.
- Named causes with "ruled out" lines, and creative ranked by angle → hook → format, capped at medium unless store-joined.
- Calls with "would change if", "what to make next" with producers, and 3 proposed learnings in a fixed template.

An analytics lead would ship it.
- MINOR: the first-run asks (13 months of weekly store totals, 5 weeks of ad exports, Klaviyo and CRM CSVs) are where founders drop off. The playbook does "work with what arrives", but it doesn't name the minimum that still yields a useful read.
- **Fix:** define and lead with the minimum viable read.

### 13. email-lifecycle — 4.5
**Run (Shopify + Klaviyo skincare store, 12k list):**
- A deliverability report: DMARC red (verified by `dig`), spam rate unknown, Shopify's native abandoned-checkout email flagged as a duplicate.
- Abandoned checkout and welcome, in real Klaviyo syntax: flow filters, smart sending, coupon pool, out-of-stock fallbacks, a holdout placed after the code is delivered.
- Margin math beside the 10% code, a priority map and cap, a month calendar and this week's broadcasts.

A lifecycle lead would load it unedited; this is the deepest playbook in the set.
- MINOR (tokens, not output): SKILL.md is 3.4k words. Step 2 alone is ~400 words of plan-reality and exclusion edge cases (waitlist reminders, click-as-conversion stand-ins). They are correct, but they belong in the references the step already loads.
- **Fix:** move step 2's edge cases into the references.

### 14. improve-search-visibility — 4
**Run (restaurant-scheduling SaaS):**
- 14 agreed questions.
- A report: "named 0 of 3 ChatGPT runs for 'best scheduling app for restaurants'; 7shifts and Homebase named; cites G2 and two listicles".
- Five finished fixes: G2 category fields, the Crunchbase description, a pricing-page answer block, an inclusion-request email, Search Console verification.
- A "7shifts alternatives" page brief.

The citation protocol and honest-claims rules are senior.
- MINOR: Pillar 4's core outputs are a prioritised roadmap and a keyword/question-to-page map. This playbook produces a weekly tactical slice, and the question set has no demand weighting (no volume data exists, but Search Console impressions could rank questions when shared).
- MINOR: in hosted chat the human runs 20 pastes, and "is there an app, is it live?" is asked although the public Apple lookup answers it.
- **Fix:** a standing cluster → page map with winnability and priority, read and updated each run.

### 15. work-with-creators — 4
**Run (DTC dog joint chews, $5k):**
- A plan: 20 seeding micros, 3 paid, 2 UGC-for-ads.
- A roster with median views, comment reads, competitor-conflict checks, and floor/target/walk-away math with organic and rights-amplified ceilings.
- Gift/paid/hybrid per creator, one-page claims-safe briefs, and near-misses with numbers.

The pricing math is better than most agencies'.
- MINOR: it stops before the step where creator programs actually stall, the first message. A drafted first-touch DM/email per pick (the human sends it) is low-risk and high-leverage, and is not "outreach" in the D4 sense.
- MINOR: no "how we'll judge wave 1" block (codes, links, what counts as a win per role, renew/cut rule), so analyze-performance keeps creators as a future row.
- **Fix:** a first-touch draft per pick plus wave-1 criteria.

### 16. make-content — 4

A content lead would edit lightly.

A social lead would post days 1–3 with light edits.

## Cross-set findings

1. **The claims gate is right, but its route out is wrong (MAJOR, affects static, video, content, social, creators).**
   - The normal first-run state is "no sign-off". Every maker then ships product facts only and tells the human to go to learn-my-brand and come back.
   - For ads, that means no benefit line: a senior would rewrite the set.
   - The gate isn't the problem; the switch is. Make `claims-audit` a shared atom any maker can run inline for the 3–5 load-bearing lines. It writes through learn-my-brand's exact contract (same provenance, same "Claims you may use" facts, same marker), so sign-off happens in the flow without loosening it.
   - This is the consolidation pass's most valuable extraction.

2. **SKILL.md bloat comes from the critique loop.**
   - SKILL.md files run 1,700–3,600 words (AGENTS §5 asks for short).
   - Critique rounds 3–7 were mostly about plumbing (dates, paging, re-runs, marker order, save order), and each fix landed inline in a process step: listen-social step 4, email-lifecycle step 2, learn-my-brand step 7, research-competitors step 2, make-static-ads "Engine facts".
   - The craft correctly lives in references; the plumbing should too.
   - Rule for the consolidation pass: a SKILL step says the marketer's move and the decision it produces, plus one line naming the atom that carries the protocol.

3. **The same boilerplate is repeated across all 17.**
   - Connection, `get_started`, `tools_load` and brand selection.
   - The claims-contract read and the "learnings in full, check `learnings_total`" dance.
   - Workspace scope with the null-coworker fallback, and the credits-log and probe protocol.
   - That is ~300–600 words each. It belongs in the foundation data-access skill, and is already partly noted in individual critiques.

4. **Inventory without decision.** The research and planning outputs are rich, but the "so what" call is uneven:
   - research-market doesn't rank segments;
   - improve-search-visibility has no roadmap or page map;
   - plan-campaign buries its decision;
   - research-competitors' hand-over is closest to right ("three things that matter most").

   Senior work always ends with "do this first, because". Add a one-paragraph recommendation as the first line of every research and plan artifact.

5. **Asks the human what a senior would find themselves.**
   - where-to-start: forbids a glance at the site, store or ads.
   - run-organic-social and make-content: ask for 10–20 pasted LinkedIn/X posts though proxy reads exist; the two siblings disagree with research-audience's data map.
   - improve-search-visibility: asks whether an app exists and is live (the public Apple lookup answers it).
   - email-lifecycle: asks for the revenue event even when the brand type and campaign brief imply it. It is prefilled, so this one is minor.

   Tool-gap asks (pasted pricing pages in hosted chat, AI-answer pastes, G2 by ToS) are legitimate and named honestly.

6. **Guides vs playbooks.**
   - No playbook is only a guide.
   - **where-to-start** is the most guide-like: pure reasoning over reads, and its unique value is the playbook map. That map should become foundation-router knowledge.
   - Parts of **make-content** (`formats.md` anatomies) and **run-organic-social** (`calendar-craft` mix ratios, `platform-native` norms) restate what the model knows. They cost tokens and improve output slightly. Trim to the non-obvious items (slide-2 re-serve, link placement, commercial-music rule, half-lives).

7. **D2 over-constraint spots (rulebooks for judgment).**
   - Verbatim-only claims, outside regulated categories. "Free shipping over $35" doesn't need a listed-variant mechanism to become "free shipping on $35+".
   - make-static-ads' "≥3 awareness stages or the set fails".
   - research-audience's freshness multipliers.

   Each is defensible locally; together they push the model toward checklist output. Let the gate be "no new meaning", not "no new words", for low-risk fact and offer claims.

8. **Loops that don't close.**
   - Paid closes cleanly: makers tag `angle:A-NN`, analyze-performance reads it, run-paid acts on it, and the angle bank updates its test status.
   - Organic, creators and lifecycle don't. Their results go to analyze-performance's "light" rows or "future rows", and nothing returns to reweight the mix, the roster or the flow refresh at the depth a senior would.
   - Each of the three needs a named hypothesis per cycle and a read-back step.
