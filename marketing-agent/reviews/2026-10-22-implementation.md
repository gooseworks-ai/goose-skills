# Marketing gap implementation — 22 October 2026

The agreed focused outputs are implemented in the existing fourteen playbooks and shared steps. Battlecards and launch positioning replace their old dedicated jobs. Other broader or raw interfaces stay available where complete replacement was not proved. No production deployment or paid collection was performed.

## What changed

- Competitor research: sales battlecards, landing-page technology evidence and source-linked LinkedIn engagers.
- Market research: positioning choices and finished messages; only an owner-selected choice becomes the current working brief.
- Brand voice: company-blog samples produce a sourced shared voice guide. Content/social consume it; campaign/content/outreach consume selected positioning.
- Search: requested audit scope/count, topic/ranking gap matrix, subject clusters, programmatic page-set blueprints and native, source-labelled domain metrics.
- Focused requests: raw social reads and multiple supplied-topic briefs skip unrelated campaign/calendar intake. Direct product updates and ordinary survey reports need no dedicated public job. Outreach remains copy plus import file; the user sends.
- Shared data guidance matches the app’s managed describe/quote/lookup contract and existing provider access. Missing data stays missing.

## Evidence

Source validation, the generated catalog, all 56 automated source tests, and the installed-layout check (86 marketing skills, 669 references) passed. Independent source review found and verified repairs to voice/positioning handoffs and native-metric fixture expectations.

Thirteen substantive example outputs are supplied beside the relevant skills: five research/voice outputs and eight SEO reports. They are manual executions against fictional, supplied source evidence. The SEO checker verifies their source fields, counts, exclusions and partial cases. These are not fresh-model execution or live provider proof.

Battlecard and positioning outputs preserve the agreed old output essentials, so their old folders are archived under `archive/retired-gtm/` and the retirement manifest names active replacements. The retained page-intelligence API, blog extraction interface, general HTML asset renderer and video bridges cover broader/raw contracts and remain available.

## What remains unverified

Paid provider results and actual charges, brand/workspace saves, live rendered-page extraction and finished media renders were not exercised. The app’s free metadata and bounded quote paths were checked separately. A separate external Claude fixture run was rejected by automatic approval review over possible export of internal source; local evidence checks were used instead. No approval bypass was attempted.

Review surfaces are this source PR, the app/CLI companion PRs and local desktop/mobile screenshots. Merging or deploying still requires explicit user approval.
