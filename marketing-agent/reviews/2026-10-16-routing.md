# Routing and run-ability critique: 17 marketing-agent playbooks

**Scope.** `marketing-agent/playbooks/*/` in the `marketing-agent-playbooks` worktree: SKILL.md frontmatter and body, atoms, references, `skill.meta.json`. Checked against AGENTS.md §2 and §5 and the GooseWorks server instructions. Read-only; `skills/` and `parts/` were not opened.

## Summary

- **Three playbooks have broken YAML frontmatter and may not load at all** (MAJOR): `analyze-performance`, `plan-campaign`, `run-paid-campaigns`. Each description starts with a double-quoted phrase and keeps going after the closing quote (`"…next?" · "write my Monday report" · …`). A strict YAML parser rejects that. These three own the most common paid-media asks.
- **Description length passes AGENTS §5.** All 17 are ≤ 1,536 characters. The longest is `email-lifecycle` at 1,351 (parsed). Nine are over 1,024 characters. That is the Agent Skills spec limit and the Claude API upload limit, so they would fail there if ever published as plain skills.
- **Seven common asks route to the wrong playbook or tie** (MAJOR): "my ad got rejected", "we sold out", "kill the losers", "research my audience", "why did sales drop", "write a LinkedIn post / turn this blog into posts", and "I need UGC". Two more common asks have no owner and land on the wrong playbook: cold email sequences and Google search ad copy. Fixes are below.
- **No broken paths.** Every `references/…`, `atoms/…` and `../../references/…` path in all 17 SKILL.md files and all 66 atom files exists. Every `gooseworks:<tool>` named exists on the live server, except `gooseworks:tools_load`. That tool is on staging but not on the production tool list this session sees. Every call to it is guarded ("if a tool is not listed"), so it is harmless.
- **Structure is good.** All 17 follow the §5 section order and have the one-sentence "Connect GooseWorks" stop. All 17 `skill.meta.json` files parse and have every §5 field, and their `atoms` lists match the `atoms/` folders exactly.
- **User-facing language mostly follows the server rules, with a few leaks** (MINOR). Some scripted lines name playbook slugs or internal ids (R14, E1, T1). One playbook says to show USD ceilings. One shows the agent's own credit sum. Two ask technical questions.

**Fix for the three YAML failures:** switch to the `>-` block form that `listen-social` and `research-competitors` already use. It needs no escaping:
