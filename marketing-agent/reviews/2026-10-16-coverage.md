# Coverage critique: the 17 marketing-agent playbooks

Audited 2026-10-16 against page 00 (day-one inputs and 22 pillars) and page 01 (process per pillar). I judged the playbooks as written, not their specs. Paths are relative to `marketing-agent/playbooks/` unless they start with `/` or `plan/`.

## Summary

1. **The DTC-on-Meta loop is real and closes.** It runs research → plan → static, video and photos → run (Meta levers plus cards) → measure against store revenue → learnings → monthly replan. Ad rejection, stock-out, competitor moves and mention spikes each have a written reactive branch.
2. **Two of the five simulated companies are stuck in week 1.** The Amazon seller has nothing for listings, Amazon ads (PPC) or measurement. The multi-location local business has no weekly review work. The B2B SaaS founder gets stuck at every step that turns research into selling.
3. **Four gaps are on no backlog at all:** paid-search ad copy and keywords; landing-page, homepage and product-page copy; sales collateral; and an owner for the weekly operating loop.
4. **Four backlog decisions are wrong for the stated ICP** (marketers at any kind of company): `optimize-listings`, `write-my-positioning`, the writing half of `do-outreach`, and `manage-reviews`. `plan-offers` is overdue by its own trigger: Q4 promo season is now, and Black Friday is 27 Nov.
5. **The loop closes on paper through workspace files, but only if a human drives it.** Every Monday someone must run six playbooks in the right order with fresh exports. Scheduled runs are unordered, and without exports the measurement step drops to "claims-only" (platform-reported numbers only).

## MAJOR findings (an ICP marketer hits this in month one and is stuck)

### M1. Amazon and other marketplace sellers are effectively unserved
- **What's missing:** listing audit and copy (title, bullets, A+ content, backend keywords), the listing image set, managing Sponsored Products (harvesting converting terms, negative keywords, bids against ACoS), a marketplace yardstick for the weekly report, review and Vine work, and deals and coupons. The same unbuilt card holds DTC catalogue and feed health (Meta catalogue, Merchant Center disapprovals, TikTok Shop).
- **Who hits it:** an Amazon seller, from week 1. A Shopify + Meta DTC brand hits it the first time a feed disapproval stops its catalogue ads.
- **Evidence:**
  - `where-to-start/references/situation-lens.md:80` and `playbook-map.md:167` know only the "two-sided marketplace" type. An Amazon seller is not a recognised type.
  - `analyze-performance/references/measurement-contract.md:7-15`: the yardstick rows (the "referee") cover DTC, B2B, app and local only. The export cheat sheet has no Seller Central or Amazon Ads export, so the weekly report has no real number to judge by.
  - `plan-campaign/references/company-types.md:43-46` sets a TACoS goal chain. But `run-paid-campaigns/references/written-instructions.md` has click paths only for Google, TikTok, LinkedIn, Meta and Apple Search Ads, and no Amazon moves.
  - `learn-my-brand/SKILL.md:32` needs a website or App Store link to set up the brand. `make-static-ads/SKILL.md:29` says a brand with no site can't generate images, so an Amazon-only seller gets no ads.
  - Retail-media creative is "not covered yet" (`make-static-ads/references/formats-and-placements.md:41`, `make-video-ads/references/angles-formats-and-company-types.md:51`). Listing copy is excluded in `make-content/SKILL.md:125`.
  - The backlog reason, "next frontier after launch; big build", is not a trigger.
- **Fix:** split `optimize-listings` and build a text-and-audit v1 now:
  - Listing audit from a Seller Central export or paste against the top three rivals.
  - Listing copy under the marketplace claims rules (`learn-my-brand/references/category-rules.md` already has them).
  - The main and secondary image shot list through `make-product-photos`.
  - Feed health from Merchant Center and catalogue exports.

  Then add a marketplace referee and Amazon exports to `analyze-performance`, Sponsored Products moves to `run-paid-campaigns`' cards, and the seller type to `where-to-start`. Feed sync and deal automation stay in the backlog.

### M2. Offer and promo design is overdue by its own trigger
- **What's missing:** designing an offer backwards from margin across all channels, a yearly promo calendar, a code plan with stacking and leakage checks before launch, a per-channel promo brief, the mid-promo kill-or-extend call, a post-mortem on extra revenue against margin given away, and giveaways and loyalty.
- **Who hits it:** the DTC skincare brand today. It is 16 Oct and Black Friday is 27 Nov (`where-to-start/references/situation-lens.md` §7). Local businesses (new-patient offer), apps (trial offers) and Amazon sellers (coupons, deals) hit it too.
- **Evidence:**
  - The backlog card says "sequence to land before the Q4 promo season". That trigger has fired.
  - `where-to-start/references/playbook-map.md:146` tells the founder: "Offer design isn't covered yet. Decide the offer."
  - The interim covers less than it sounds:
    - `email-lifecycle/atoms/offer-math` only prices the standing offer or a ladder the human already approved. Anything else is a proposal, and only for email.
    - `plan-campaign/references/budget-and-tests.md:15` costs only "an offer the founder named".
    - `plan-campaign/SKILL.md:70` points back to plan-offers.
- **Fix:** build `plan-offers` now, at minimum the promo-brief half:
  - goal → mechanic → depth from margin, plus a forecast;
  - collisions with other promos, the code plan and a checklist for checking the offer at the cart;
  - one brief for every channel;
  - a post-mortem section that `analyze-performance` reads.

  Loyalty and referral design can follow.

### M3. Nobody writes landing pages, homepages, product pages or popups
- **What's missing:** campaign landing pages, product-page copy blocks, homepage and pricing-page copy, popup and form offer copy, and waitlist pages.
- **Who hits it:**
  - DTC: the Black Friday landing page, a bundle product page, the popup that grows the list.
  - B2B SaaS: the homepage and pricing page, which are where positioning lives.
  - App: the waitlist page.
  - Paid: every paid launch, because the destination has to match the ad.
- **Evidence:**
  - `plan-campaign/references/brief-template.md:81` sends "PDP / landing-page copy" to "human-executed until a maker covers them". `plan-campaign/server-needs.md:19` (#13) records the same gap.
  - `plan-campaign/references/launch-and-calendar.md` budgets "Landing page 1–2 weeks", but names no producer.
  - `run-paid-campaigns/references/launch-and-graduate.md` (health gate, "Destination") blocks launches on a page mismatch.
  - `make-content/SKILL.md:125` and `references/formats.md` ("Out of the format list") exclude these pages. `email-lifecycle/SKILL.md` Out of scope routes popups to `audit-my-pages`.
  - `audit-my-pages` is framed as a conversion audit triggered by use. But the need to write pages comes from every launch, not from analysis.
- **Fix:** add page formats to `make-content` now: section structure, copy, a claims check and image notes, with the human building the page. Keep conversion testing as the `audit-my-pages` backlog card.

### M4. Positioning from scratch: the backlog decision is wrong for a $0 brand
- **What's missing:** a positioning statement, a messaging framework per segment, an elevator pitch, boilerplate and a brand story.
- **Who hits it:**
  - A just-launched B2B SaaS, in week 1.
  - A services firm: positioning is its heaviest pillar (page 00, Part 4).
  - Any pre-launch app.
- **Evidence:**
  - The backlog card's reason, "Founders often arrive with no positioning", is always true for this ICP, so it is not a trigger.
  - `where-to-start/references/playbook-map.md:145` tells the founder: "You decide the positioning".
  - `learn-my-brand/SKILL.md` Out of scope records only the owner's own one-liner, or leaves it unknown.
  - `make-content/SKILL.md:30` says "I'll write around them".
  - `research-competitors` produces white space (`landscape.md`), and its readers list includes `write-my-positioning (backlog)`. So the white space feeds campaign angles but never becomes a positioning decision.
- **Fix:** build `write-my-positioning` now. Every input already exists: the voice-of-customer bank, the segments, the landscape and the angle bank. It should output:
  - 2–3 positioning options for the founder to choose from;
  - a messaging framework with every proof line traced to a signed-off claim;
  - boilerplate and the homepage hero lines;
  - a cheap message test through `make-static-ads`' angle test;
  - a write-back through `learn-my-brand`'s claims gate.

### M5. No playbook writes paid-search ads
- **What's missing:** search-ad copy (Google's responsive search ads: up to 15 headlines and 4 descriptions), sitelinks and callouts, keyword themes and match types, starter negative keywords, and keywords for Local Services Ads and Apple Search Ads.
- **Who hits it:**
  - B2B SaaS: intent, competitor and "alternatives" searches.
  - Local: "near me" searches and Local Services Ads.
  - DTC: brand and category search.

  `plan-campaign` routinely marks Google Search **Run** for these businesses (`references/channel-decision.md:38`, `company-types.md`).
- **Evidence:**
  - `plan-campaign/server-needs.md:19` says "No maker for search ad copy, keyword lists". It routes them to the human in `brief-template.md:81`.
  - `run-paid-campaigns` route 3 expects search-ad copy "from the brief's asset row once the human confirmed it" (`references/launch-and-graduate.md`).
  - `make-static-ads` makes image ads only.
  - The gap is on no backlog card and in no `where-to-start` gap row.
- **Fix:** add a search-ads mode to `make-static-ads`, which already owns ad copy, the claims check and the policy check:
  - search-ad copy per ad group;
  - keyword themes built from `research-market`'s demand read and customers' own words;
  - starter negatives.

  `run-paid-campaigns` launches it as cards and already handles search-term negatives each week.

### M6. Prospect lists dead-end: no outreach sequences
- **What's missing:** 4–6 touch email and LinkedIn sequences per segment, personalised first lines, reply and objection templates, follow-up rules and a domain warm-up checklist.
- **Who hits it:**
  - A B2B SaaS founder in weeks 2–3, holding 50 rows and no words.
  - Services firms.
  - CPG brands pitching retail buyers.
  - Marketplaces recruiting supply that is a business.
- **Evidence:**
  - `research-audience/SKILL.md:80` (Out of scope) and the `do-outreach` card ("build when a sending layer exists") send both writing and sending to the backlog.
  - `plan/marketing-twin/decisions-and-parking-lot.md:44` repeats that do-outreach waits on a sending layer.
  - D4 split the same problem for social: drafts now, posting parked (`run-organic-social`).
  - `analyze-performance/references/creative-and-channels.md` keeps outreach as a "future row".
  - `research-audience` already produces hooks and a "why we're writing" line per segment.
- **Fix:** split `do-outreach` and build the writing half now: sequences, LinkedIn notes and reply templates, with a claims check. The human sends and pastes outcomes into `research-audience`'s outcomes intake. Sending and reply handling stay in the backlog.

### M7. No one makes sales collateral, and it is on no backlog
- **What's missing:** one-pagers, battlecards, objection-handling FAQs, demo scripts, sell-sheets, line sheets and proposals.
- **Who hits it:**
  - A B2B founder before the first sales calls.
  - A services firm writing proposals.
  - A DTC + retail or CPG brand pitching the buyers that `research-audience` just listed.
- **Evidence:**
  - `plan-campaign/references/company-types.md:34` says enablement assets (one-pager, battlecard, demo script) "go on the asset list for any launch". But `make-content/SKILL.md:125` and `references/formats.md` exclude them ("not v1").
  - `research-audience/SKILL.md:80`: "Sell-sheets, line sheets and pitch one-pagers — no playbook owns them yet (... flagged in critique.md for the map)". The map never absorbed this.
  - The map marks pillar 10 "covered — plan-campaign", which covers launches only.
- **Fix:** add enablement formats to `make-content`: words plus layout notes. Battlecards come from `research-competitors` files, objection handling from the voice-of-customer bank, and sell-sheets lead with velocity and margin. Give decks their own backlog card with a trigger.

### M8. Review management: the backlog trigger isn't a trigger
- **What's missing:** a weekly reply queue for each location or surface, rating and review-velocity watch, and reactive handling of a 1-star storm.
- **Who hits it:**
  - The multi-location local business in week 1: reviews are its top ranking and conversion lever.
  - Apps after a release drops the rating.
  - Amazon sellers.
  - DTC brands on Trustpilot.
- **Evidence:**
  - The `manage-reviews` card's "build when" reads "Name it in page 00 first; small build". That is a to-do, not a trigger.
  - `listen-social/references/queries-and-coverage.md:50`: Google Business Profile, Yelp, App Store, Amazon, G2 and Trustpilot are "not on the listening path — paste".
  - `improve-search-visibility/references/entity-and-local.md:30` drafts replies only inside its own (monthly) run.
  - `email-lifecycle` asks customers for reviews, but nothing answers them.
- **Fix:** build `manage-reviews` now, since it is small:
  - a weekly reply queue from exports or paste;
  - safety items escalated through `listen-social`'s crisis triage;
  - review requests left in `email-lifecycle`.

  Or absorb it into `listen-social` as a "reviews" surface.

### M9. The weekly operating loop has no owner
- **What's missing:** one Monday run that:
  - collects the week's exports once;
  - sequences analyze → paid moves → rotation requests → email weekly → organic refill → listening and competitor digests;
  - ends with one calendar of everything going out across channels.

  A day-one inventory of accounts and tool access is also missing.
- **Who hits it:** every company from week 2.
- **Evidence:**
  - AGENTS.md rule 9 says workflows never call other playbooks. `foundation/` today holds only `data-access`; the router isn't built.
  - Nine playbooks each offer their own automation, with nothing setting the order. Yet three of them read last week's `analyze-performance` report:
    - `run-paid-campaigns/SKILL.md` Before-you-start 6, and its recurring offer at line 55;
    - `email-lifecycle/references/modes-and-incidents.md:9`;
    - `run-organic-social/SKILL.md:42`.
  - `run-organic-social` offers no recurring run.
  - `analyze-performance/references/measurement-contract.md:70-71`: a scheduled run is claims-only unless a connector exists, and `analyze-performance/server-needs.md` #1–3 says none does.
  - Parking-lot item P2 covers schedules and watchers, but not sequencing or a single intake.
- **Fix:** give the weekly sequence to the router or a "run-my-week" foundation skill. Schedule it as one automation with the order fixed; it also owns the cross-channel "going out this week" calendar (pillar 14). Watchers stay in P2.

## MINOR findings (real, but rarer or partly covered)

- **m1. A brand crisis is handled only on social.**
  - **Gap:** `listen-social` drafts replies and holding statements, and `run-organic-social` has a crisis pause (`references/calendar-craft.md:110`). But neither `run-paid-campaigns`' reactive entries (`SKILL.md:36-38`) nor `email-lifecycle`'s incident table (`references/modes-and-incidents.md:27-35`) has a brand or recall incident. A DTC brand facing a viral breakout complaint keeps running its "gentle" ads and promo emails.
  - **Fix:** a "brand incident (listen-social S1)" row in both, plus a cross-channel hold list in the S1 output.
- **m2. Multi-location gaps beyond reviews.**
  - **Gap:**
    - The weekly report has no per-location split (`analyze-performance/references/creative-and-channels.md:59`).
    - Weekly Google Business Profile posts per location have no recurring home: `run-organic-social` Out of scope sends them to a `make-content` format only.
    - Per-location records have no structure (`learn-my-brand/server-needs.md` §10).
    - Copy for flyers, local partner pitches and referral cards has no maker.
    - The pillar 20 skip reason ("off-ICP weight") contradicts D3.
  - **Fix:** per-location rows (bookings against capacity), Business Profile slots in the calendar, the local formats in `make-content`, and a corrected skip reason. Physical execution stays human.
- **m3. Plan rows with no executor and no backlog card.**
  - **Gap:** communities (`plan-campaign/references/company-types.md:29` lists Reddit and Slack groups, while `run-organic-social` excludes posting there), affiliate programmes (`work-with-creators` Out of scope; on no backlog), a Product Hunt launch kit, and webinar invites and follow-up.
  - **Fix:** backlog cards for affiliates and community, each with a trigger. The Product Hunt and webinar kits become `make-content` formats.
- **m4. Carousels and slideshows can't be made.**
  - **Gap:** `run-organic-social/SKILL.md:92` forbids `render`/`carousel`, and `make-static-ads`' graphics side door makes single images only. Slideshows are a top format for consumer apps on TikTok and B2B founders on LinkedIn.
  - **Fix:** a credit-gated carousel render in `make-static-ads`' graphic-request mode, separate from publishing.
- **m5. B2B content and SEO lacks a programme owner.**
  - **Gap:**
    - There is no topic or page roadmap. `improve-search-visibility` has no keyword-research atom, though the map lists one; its atoms are citation-check, entity-footprint, fix-writer, page-and-foundation-check, question-set and store-listing-audit.
    - `make-content` leaves topic choice to the human.
    - `analyze-performance` only nudges a re-check and has no organic-search or content row.
  - **Fix:** a quarterly page roadmap from `improve-search-visibility`, and a Search Console row in the weekly report.
- **m6. The PR skip is partly wrong.**
  - **Gap:** a launch press release, a short targeted media list and gift-guide pitches are research plus drafts, the same pattern D4 approved for creators. AI-answer citations depend on earned media. Yet `research-audience` refuses journalist lists, and `plan-campaign/references/launch-and-calendar.md` budgets gift guides at 3–5 months' lead with no producer.
  - **Fix:** a research-and-drafts PR card with the trigger "first T1 launch or first DTC Q4 plan", and a press-release format in `make-content` meanwhile.
- **m7. The WhatsApp trigger isn't a trigger.**
  - **Gap:** "market-dependent" doesn't say when. `email-lifecycle/references/sms-push-whatsapp.md` itself names India, Southeast Asia and Brazil as markets where WhatsApp is the primary channel.
  - **Fix:** trigger on the first brand whose main market is one of these.
