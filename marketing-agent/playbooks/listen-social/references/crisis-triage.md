# Crisis triage

Load at step 4 (risk first). Any item that could hurt someone or the brand is triaged the moment it is seen — not after the rest of the pass.

## Severity

| Level | What qualifies | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **S1** | safety or health harm attributed to the product when **any** of: the post has ≥50k views within 24 hours, the poster has ≥100k followers, or ≥3 separate people report the same harm; contamination, injury or a recall; legal or regulatory action; a data breach; discrimination or employee misconduct; anything already picked up by press. A public-figure founder makes press pickup likely — count that toward S1. | **Stop the pass.** Put it at the top of your reply in chat, before anything else. Name the escalation contact (operating rules, else the watchlist header), or say "no escalation contact on record — decide now". Draft the on-post reply and the private message; draft the holding statement too, marked for posting only if the brand-wide conditions below hold, otherwise "held — post only if it spreads (trigger: …)". Then finish the pass. |
| **S2** | a credible complaint gaining speed (engagement per hour well above the poster's own median, replies piling up, stitches or quote-posts) below the S1 thresholds, or a complaint from a high-reach poster | Post it in chat before continuing if it is moving fast; Act-now block of the report; on-post reply + private draft; escalation note. |
| **S3** | isolated, low-reach complaint | Normal reply queue. |

For every S1–S2 flag record: the quoted text and link, the poster and their reach, velocity (engagement now vs at first sight, or vs the poster's median), how many separate people are saying it (use the crisis reserve: comments on the flagged post, a search for repeats), whether the claim is checkable and whether the poster appears to be a customer (only the human can check order data), and what we know vs do not know. Adverse reactions are also logged for the brand's adverse-event process (the human passes them on).

**Cross-channel hold list (S1).** Paid ads keep running and promo emails keep sending while a crisis builds. For every S1, list what touches the product at risk so the human can pause it: live paid ads (`gooseworks:meta_read` shows the ads GooseWorks launched; ask about any others), posts scheduled in `social/calendar/`, and promo emails or flows in `lifecycle/` — each with where it runs. This playbook pauses nothing; it is a recommendation for the human.

## Holding statement vs on-post reply

- A **brand-wide holding statement** (posted on the brand's own feed) is recommended for posting only when 3 or more separate people raise the issue, press has picked it up, or contamination, a recall or legal action is confirmed by the brand, a regulator or the press. One person's reported reaction or injury is a single-poster item; posted for one person's complaint — however big their reach — a statement amplifies it.
- **Any single-poster item** (S1 or S2) gets a reply on that post, a private-message draft and escalation now; its holding statement waits, drafted, with the trigger that would release it.
- Open items stay open: re-read them each run (views, separate posters, stitches) and keep them in Act now as "open since <date>" until the human closes them.

A holding statement's purpose: acknowledge, show the brand is on it, move people to a private channel, buy time for facts. It must:
- acknowledge the concern in plain words;
- say the brand is looking into it now;
- give a time when people will hear more (the human sets it);
- point affected people to a support channel.

It must never: deny, blame anyone, speculate on cause (including "purging", "detox", "an adjustment period" or any other explanation), admit fault or liability, use legal language, promise refunds or compensation beyond the policy in the brain, mention batch numbers or details before facts are checked, name staff, or argue.

## What the agent never does

Reply, DM, comment, hide or delete comments, report posts, contact the poster, contact press or platforms, or post anything. It drafts and recommends; the human decides and sends.

## Be honest about timing

A weekly pass catches crises late. Whenever an S1 or S2 appears, say so, and mention that always-on alerts aren't available yet. A standing weekly run writes its findings to the workspace; nobody sees them until someone opens the report — it is not an alert.
