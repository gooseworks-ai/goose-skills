# The channel decision

Load at step 4. The output is the brief's **Channel decisions** section: every candidate channel gets a verdict, the one reason that decides it, the brand fact behind it, and — for anything not running — what would flip it. This is judgment, not a scoring formula. The lenses are what a senior planner asks; the playbook only requires that each verdict names its binding lens and cites a brand fact.

## Verdicts

- **Run** — gets real budget or real hours in this plan.
- **Test** — gets a capped, time-boxed test with success criteria (see `budget-and-tests.md`).
- **Later** — fits the brand, not this plan; name the flip condition.
- **No** — does not fit the brand; one line why.

Supporting legs (organic social posting alongside a paid launch) are **Run** with a note "supporting".

## The seven lenses (one sentence each, citing a brand input)

1. **Audience and intent** — are these buyers there, and in buying mode? Check the people a channel reaches are the **buyer**, not peers, builders or competitors (a founder's X following may be other founders; the buyer may live on LinkedIn or Instagram). If the marketer picked a channel that reaches someone else, say it once, plainly, then follow their choice. Demand capture (they search: Google Search, Shopping, Amazon, App Store, AI assistants) vs. demand creation (they scroll: Meta, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, CTV). A new category with no search volume cannot be captured.
2. **Footprint** — can the click land where the product is sold? Amazon-only brands gain little from site-traffic ads; a brand stocked in Target needs retail media or a store locator; an app needs install-capable campaigns.
3. **Economics** — does the leg's minimum viable spend fit the budget, and is expected CPA plausible against the allowable CAC? A budget too small for two channels is a one-channel plan.
4. **Readiness** — can we supply what it needs: creative volume and formats, a clean product feed, conversion tracking, an email list, enough reviews/social proof?
5. **Time-to-signal** — can it produce a readable result before the date? SEO (3–6 months) and creator programmes (3–6 weeks lead) often fail this for a 6-week launch.
6. **Evidence** — the brand's own history and learnings log, research outputs (persona, angle bank), whether competitors invest here (competitor file). A channel the learnings log says failed is "No" unless something material changed; name it.
7. **Execution** — who pulls the levers? Meta via run-paid-campaigns: pushing Goose creatives into an existing ad set, or a new Traffic-objective test campaign with a lifetime budget; every other Meta setup (new purchase/lead/install campaigns, radius) is written instructions, or changes through the marketer's own ads connector (run-paid-campaigns' connector rule). On a sign-up or sales goal, a Meta leg run through GooseWorks is priced as Traffic, or "the marketer sets up a conversion campaign" is a dated step (`unit-economics.md` §3). Every other paid platform: run-paid-campaigns stages written instructions the marketer applies in their own ads manager (accepted interim), or connector changes. Organic, creators, outreach: the marketer posts, contacts and closes. Count the marketer hours this costs (see "founder hours" below).

Craft rules that sit above the lenses:
- **One or two channels done properly beat five done thinly.** A plan that runs everything has not decided.
- **Stage first.** Pre-launch or no-proof brands earn attention (list, organic, creators, community, PR) before buying it; growth brands buy what already converts.
- **Owned before paid.** The list and existing customers are the cheapest launch audience.
- **Novelty is not a reason.** CTV and AI-assistant ads are never "Run" because they are new.
- **Default to nothing.** No channel is assumed — Meta included.

## Candidate rows — every mandatory channel is decided

Every mandatory channel gets a verdict. Those that are plausible for the brand get a full row; those that clearly don't apply (LinkedIn for a two-person skincare brand, CTV on $8k, retail media when not stocked) fold into **one "Not relevant:" line**, each with its reason — a named rejection without a table row.

Mandatory (always a row, even when the answer is one line of "No"):

| Channel | Usually fits | Usually doesn't | Practical minimum to learn (rule of thumb) | Creative it needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta (FB/IG)** | Visual consumer products, apps, local, broad B2C | Very narrow B2B; heavy ad-policy categories | ~50 optimisation events per ad set per week to exit learning → weekly ad-set budget ≈ 50 × target CPA. Below that: one campaign, broad targeting, or optimise a higher-funnel event | 3–5 angles × 2–3 variants; statics + 9:16 video; ~8–15 net-new creatives per month per ~$10k spend |
| **Google Search** | Category/problem is searched; B2B intent terms; local "near me" | New category with no volume; impulse products | Enough daily budget for 10+ clicks/day on core terms; tCPA bidding needs ~30 conversions / 30 days | Keyword-matched copy; landing page that answers the query |
| **Google Shopping / Performance Max** | E-commerce with a clean feed and searched products | No approved feed; services | Feed health first; PMax wants ~30+ conversions / month | Feed titles and images; asset groups |
| **LinkedIn** | B2B with a definable title/company audience and ACV that carries $80–250+ per lead | Consumer; low-ACV B2B | CPCs often $5–15+, CPMs $30–80+; below ~$3–5k/month little is learned | Proof-led statics, document ads, founder video |
| **TikTok** | Under-35 consumer, demonstrable products, apps, TikTok Shop | B2B; brands that cannot make native video weekly | ~$50/day campaign, ~$20/day ad group minimums; ~50 conversions/week per ad group; creative fatigues in 7–14 days | Native creator-style 9:16 video in volume |
| **CTV / streaming TV** | Brands proven elsewhere scaling reach; broad consumer; multi-location | Small budgets; no video; needs direct attribution now | Typically $5–10k+ for a readable flight (read via brand search / lift) | 15–30s TV-grade video |
| **Retail media (Amazon, Walmart, Instacart, Target)** | Product is stocked there | Not stocked there | Sponsored Products can start small; goal in ACoS/TACoS; depends on listing quality, reviews, Buy Box, stock | Listing images, A+ content, search terms |
| **Ads inside AI assistants** | Watch-list: categories people ask assistants about | Default **Later**: formats, reach, measurement immature | — | — |
| **Organic social** | Almost always (supporting); core at near-zero budget or when the founder's voice is the asset | Rarely "No" — may be "maintain" | Hours, not money: a cadence the team can keep (e.g. 3–5 posts/week) | Platform-native posts; founder voice |
| **Founder outreach (one-to-one)** | Win-back of lapsed or free users of a self-serve tool; high-value B2B accounts; small known audiences — a short personal note from the founder to the ~50 most active lapsed accounts is often the highest-yield win-back move | Large consumer lists; no founder time | Hours, not money: ~1–2 h per 25 notes; the marketer sends them | A short, honest note (what changed, a direct question); no templates that pretend to be personal |
| **Email / SMS (owned list)** | Any brand with a list; every launch | No list → the plan's job is list-building (waitlist, pre-orders, pop-up) | Free reach: list size × the brand's past send conversion | Sequence: tease → open → reminder → last call |
| **Content / SEO** | Long-horizon capture; B2B; considered purchases; AEO | Must pay back inside a short window | 3–6 months to rank; for a launch only supporting pages (landing, FAQ, comparison) | Pages, articles, FAQs |
| **Creators / influencers** | Consumer trust-and-demo products; launches; UGC supply for paid | B2B (bar niche experts); nothing to send | Gifting 10–30 micro-creators or 1–3 paid deals; 3–6 weeks lead; whitelisting/Spark ads multiplies value | Creator brief, product, usage rights |

Add the rows for **this brand's type only** (a consumer app does not also get the DTC rows unless they are live for it), else one line "not relevant: <reason>". Minimums to learn, rules of thumb: Apple Search Ads — starts small ($20–50/day) on brand and category terms; Reddit ads ~$30–50/day per ad group; Pinterest ~$20–50/day; newsletter/podcast sponsorship — one placement is directional, three+ in the same outlet is readable; Product Hunt/directories and referral — hours, not money.
- **Consumer apps:** Apple Search Ads; Google App campaigns (folds into the Google row); ASO readiness as a prerequisite.
- **B2B:** webinars/events, outbound (cold email, LinkedIn outreach — human-executed), review sites (G2/Capterra), comparison and alternatives pages, newsletter/podcast sponsorships, partnerships and co-marketing, directory launches (Product Hunt).
- **DTC / consumer:** Pinterest (home, food, fashion, weddings), YouTube (demos, considered purchases), Reddit, Snap, X, affiliates/referral, PR and product seeding, marketplaces as a sales channel, WhatsApp, direct mail.
- **Local / multi-location:** Google Business Profile, Local Services Ads, geo-radius Meta, local listings, events, flyers/OOH, referral.
- **Services:** referrals, partners, speaking, thought-leadership content, outbound; paid is often a valid "No".

## The row, written well

Columns: **Channel · Verdict · Binding lens · Reason (cites a brand fact) · Evidence · Confidence · Flip condition**

- Bad: "TikTok — doesn't fit our audience."
- Good: "TikTok — **Later** · *readiness* · Needs ~8 fresh native videos a week; we can make 3 · learnings log 2026-07: $1.2k test, 0 purchases · evidence · Flips when the creator leg yields 8+ usable videos a week."
- Good: "LinkedIn — **No** · *audience* · Our buyer is a home cook; LinkedIn targets job titles · buyer segments (customer research) · evidence · —"
- Good: "CTV — **No** · *economics* · A readable flight needs ~$5k+, the whole budget is $5k · — · judgment · Revisit above $40k/month total spend."

**Confidence**: *evidence* (brand data, research outputs, learnings) or *judgment* (category knowledge only). A judgment-only **Run** should usually be a **Test**.

Read the product's already-proposed campaign concepts (`campaign_read`) as input here — concepts tell you which angles exist and which products they sell; they do not decide channels.

## Founder hours

Organic, creators, outreach, community and every non-Meta paid leg cost the marketer's time. Each Run/Test row that needs human hands states hours per week (e.g. "LinkedIn posting: ~2 h/week, founder", "Google: 15 min/week applying staged moves"). If the total exceeds what the marketer said they have, the plan cuts a leg rather than assuming the hours appear.

## Zero-paid plans

No paid channel is a valid, complete output when the budget is below every paid leg's minimum, the brand has no proof or tracking yet, or the marketer said no ads. Write it plainly in the paid rows ("Meta — Later · economics · $600 cannot exit learning at a $30 CPA; spend it gifting 20 creators · flips at ~$1,500/week"). The asset list is then organic, email and creator assets, and the hand-off tells run-paid-campaigns there is nothing to run.
