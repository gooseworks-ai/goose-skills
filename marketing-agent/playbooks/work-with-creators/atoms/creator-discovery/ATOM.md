# creator-discovery

**Reads:** the plan (roles, platforms, tiers, geography); `brand_read` organic (the brand's and competitors' recent posts) and competitors; `social_inspiration_library` / `social_inspiration_search`; the existing roster (declined / never-again / already shortlisted); `references/discovery.md`; `references/data-sources.md`.

**Does:**
1. Free pass first: creators tagged in or behind the brand's and competitors' recent organic posts (and the founder's account for founder-led brands); the people behind saved Inspiration outliers; creators the human named. An empty Inspiration library is normal — carry on.
2. Paid pass within the discovery ceiling: brand tagged posts and brand-name search (existing fans — skip when the free feed shows no mentions; read each mention's sentiment); competitor partnership ads; problem-phrase keyword search per platform; commenters under category leaders; lookalikes; for UGC-for-ads, creators in category partnership ads and "UGC creator" profiles with portfolios.
3. Long list: one line per creator — handle, platform, source, why surfaced.
4. Quick screen on profile-level data only (active ≤14 days, on-niche, language/geography, tier inside the plan, not a minor, bio and top posts clean, not declined/never-again).
5. Rank finalists by source strength (positive existing fans > proven in category > search) and screen notes; keep 15–25 for paid/UGC roles, 30–60 for a gift-only seeding wave; mark each for full, light, UGC-rubric or B2B-branch vetting.

**Writes:** long list with screen results; finalist list with source, screen notes and vetting depth; running sum of reported charges.

**Rules:** never rank on follower count. A creator found through one viral post goes forward only as "needs consistency check".
