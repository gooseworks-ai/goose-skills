# creator-vetting

**Reads:** finalists; the persona and goal; signed-off claims and never-says (to spot conflicting past claims); competitor list; `references/vetting.md`; `references/data-sources.md`.

**Does, per finalist** — at the depth set in discovery (full, light, UGC rubric, B2B branch; see section 0 of the reference):
1. Pull the profile and the last 12–20 posts on the main platform; drop pinned, giveaway and too-fresh posts; split sponsored and organic.
2. Compute median views, view rate, engagement on the right basis for the platform, sponsored ÷ organic, mean ÷ median, cadence.
3. Read 50–100 comments across ~5 posts; record the share of generic/bot-like comments, self-descriptions, purchase questions, language and place signals.
4. Audience fit: evidence and a confidence level (high / medium / low), from what people say about themselves — quoted and counted. Audience countries only for reach/install roles, top five at most. UGC-for-ads: skip audience; judge hooks (transcripts), product handling, past brand-ad work, portfolio, turnaround, honesty risk; mark delivery "not watched" and record 2–3 video links for the human.
5. Brand safety: ~12 months of captions and a few transcripts; a name search with "scam / apology / drama / controversy" (if no search ran, the verdict says "captions and comments only"); past claims the brand could never make.
6. Conflicts: competitor partnership ads, competitor tags in the last 3–6 months.
7. Regulated and minors checks per the reference; any fail → out.
8. Write the verdict line (the shape at the end of the reference). Fails become near-misses with the number that failed.

**Writes:** a verdict per finalist with all numbers, `vetted_on` date and post links; near-misses with reasons.

**Rules:** no number, no verdict. Label all authenticity findings "signals, not verified". Never infer ethnicity, age, gender, skin tone or health from images or names. No business-contact lookups here — they wait until the human approves the names.
