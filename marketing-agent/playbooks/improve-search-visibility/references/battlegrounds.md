# Battlegrounds by business type

Load at step 1 (free pass). Guidance for the model, not rules: the brand's footprint decides. Name the surfaces that matter and the modules that do not apply, in one line, before drafting questions.

| Type | Top battlegrounds | Typical first-week fixes | Usually skip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Store (DTC) | "best X for Y" AI answers (often cite review sites, listicles, Reddit, YouTube); product and collection pages for category terms; brand questions ("[brand] reviews", "is [brand] legit") | product-page fact block (materials, sizes, price, shipping, returns stated plainly) + valid Product/Offer markup; collection-page title, H1 and answer-first intro; inclusion request to a cited list; review-profile completion; AI-crawler access check | app store, Google Business Profile (unless there are shops), Crunchbase/G2/Wikidata, most comparison pages |
| B2B SaaS | "best [category] for [segment]" (often cites G2/Capterra, vendor docs, comparison pages); "X vs Y" and "alternatives to X"; brand facts (pricing, integrations) | G2/Capterra profile and category fixes; pricing page with plain plan facts; a comparison-page brief to make-content; near-winner title rewrites from Search Console; integration-directory listings | app store, local, review stars |
| Consumer app | App Store and Play search; "best app for X" AI answers (listicles, store pages, Reddit); rating and brand questions | title, subtitle, keyword field rewrite; first three screenshot captions; landing page stating platforms, price and free tier; a rating-prompt ticket; inclusion request | local, deep site foundation (often one page), Product markup |
| Marketplace (two-sided) | templated category and city pages on Google; "where to hire / buy X" AI answers; trust surfaces (Trustpilot, app rating) | one template rewrite (title formula, fact intro, internal links) as a proposal; a noindex-or-merge proposal for thin pages with risk stated; a "fees and how it works" fact page; review-profile fix. Sample by category and city | single-page rewrites (the template is the unit), app store unless app-first |
| Local, multi-location | Google local pack and Maps via the Business Profile per location; review volume, recency and replies; "best X in [city]" AI answers (Business Profile, Yelp, local lists) | Business Profile category, hours and services fixes for the worst 2–3 locations; NAP fixes on top directories; location-page facts + LocalBusiness markup; a review-request brief to email-lifecycle; review-reply drafts. Sample per city | comparison pages, Wikidata/Crunchbase, app store, topic clusters |
| Pre-launch (any type) | who owns the category answers today and which sources they cite; name collisions (does anything else own the brand name?); the launch-day listing or landing page | Search Console + Bing Webmaster Tools verification and sitemap; a landing page that states what it is, who it's for, price and platforms in plain facts; Organization markup; the launch-day store listing draft; claiming the brand's profiles (Product Hunt, app stores, LinkedIn) | brand-accuracy questions beyond the name check, review profiles, near-winners |
| Services firm | "best [service] firm for [niche or city]" (directories like Clutch, listicles, LinkedIn); firm and founder name questions; niche service and case-study pages | service page stating who it's for, price range, process, locations; directory profile + review ask; founder bio and LinkedIn aligned with Organization/Person markup; case-study title rewrites; inclusion request | app store, Product markup, templated pages |

Source mix moves fast and differs by category (e.g. Reddit and Wikipedia shares in ChatGPT citations rose through mid-2026 then fell; in B2B SaaS vendor docs gained). Treat every source pattern as measured in this run, never assumed from this table.
