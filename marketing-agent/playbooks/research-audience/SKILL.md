# research-audience

Audience research answers, for the people a brand sells to: **who they are** (buyer groups, sized in buyers, with buying roles), **what they want**, **their problems and how we solve them**, **their words** (the customer-quote bank), **where they gather**, and **how to find and reach them**. It owns the brand's **one list of buyer groups** and the **quote bank**; market research reads them and sizes the market around them (its figures are context; group sizes are counted here), and every maker reads them. A list of named people is a separate mode, only when asked. The craft is in [references/audience-research.md](references/audience-research.md) and [references/quote-bank.md](references/quote-bank.md); this file is the order of work.

**The rule that matters most: the brand's own customers first, then free pass, report once** (AGENTS §8 research pattern). One specific question for the brand's customer data is allowed up front; after that, cover every group on every dimension from free sources before the next message. Never stop to ask "which group should I go deeper on?". Paid reads only deepen it, with one credit yes.

**Depth matches the ask, and the marketer pays for every call.** Aim for a few dollars a run:
- **Quick** ("who's our customer, roughly?", a single question): no workers, about 20 web calls in all, the answer in chat plus a one-page report.
- **Standard** ("do the audience research", the default): **at most three group workers, one level only**, each capped (segment-pass § Budget); news done by you (about 6 searches) and sizing gaps the workers left (about 6 more). About 80 web calls in all.
- **Deep** (the marketer asks for depth, or after the paid top-up yes): up to five workers; the caps stay.
Never brief a worker to load this playbook or any skill — the brief is [atoms/segment-pass](atoms/segment-pass/ATOM.md) pasted whole plus the inputs, and nothing else; workers use only web search and page reads, no GooseWorks calls. Sampling and saving the quote bank ([references/quote-bank.md](references/quote-bank.md)) are yours, not the workers'. Workers never start workers. Search before you fetch; fetch only a page you'll quote or count from.

## What you get

1. **The audience report** through [`../../atoms/write-report/ATOM.md`](../../atoms/write-report/ATOM.md) (opens with one click, saved to the brand): in short; decisions for you; the buyer groups — ranked, each sized in buyers or companies, who uses / pays / approves, the lead one named — labelled provisional when the evidence doesn't clear the bar; per group what they want, their problems and how we solve them (or "you'd need proof of X first"), their words, where they gather and how to reach them; why they buy and why they leave (from the brand's own data, where it exists); how rivals talk to them; what to do; what we're not doing; what we couldn't check; sources with a source-skew line.
2. **Buyer groups** and the **quote bank** — the durable records every other playbook reads.
3. **Only in list mode:** a list built, a list the marketer brings scored (and inbound leads ranked by who to answer first), or a standing watch — named accounts and people with dated sources, honest contact routes and a buying signal per row ([references/list-mode.md](references/list-mode.md)).

## Before you start

1. **Connection and brand.** First GooseWorks call: `gooseworks:get_started` with the marketer's words (`wants: "more"`); it settles setup and the brand only (when it asks which brand, ask in one line and call it again). A needed tool not listed → one `gooseworks:tools_load` (`brand`, `files`, `media`, `data`). Still none → say only: "Connect GooseWorks first — I research your audience and save what I find through it." Send `client` on every call that takes it. Keep `brand_id`, `brand_slug`, `coworker_agent_id`.
2. **Read the brain — free.** **When `brain_*` tools are listed:** `gooseworks:brain_map` first (core rules, the never-list, items waiting for the marketer's yes — an audience proposal from an earlier run may still be waiting), then open the audience and customer chapters it points to, 14 (rules), 22 (what we've made and run) and 23 (what is working) with `gooseworks:brain_map` `kind` or `gooseworks:brain_open`, and `gooseworks:brain_search` for customer evidence, synced reviews, pasted exports and earlier research. **Otherwise:** `gooseworks:brand_read` (summary, kit — especially `audience` — products, competitors, organic, learnings) and `gooseworks:knowledge_search` for the same. Confirmed items are the brand's truth; found ones are context, and items waiting for a yes are labelled so. `gooseworks:competitor_read` either way. Workspace, **brand coworker scope** (the `brands/<brand-slug>/` prefix only when there is no coworker): `research/audience/` (buyer groups, quote bank, last report — a re-run says what changed), `research/market/` (the latest market report — its market figures are context; group sizes are counted here), `research/competitors/` (rival records and report). Read every `outreach/*/results-working.md` (write-outreach) with open request lines — `Hold needed` (exclude those accounts until answered), `Find needed` (find that role at that account), `Quote needed` (add the phrase to the bank) — answering each under its line, and the lessons that change the fit.  **No audience bank yet but an older market one** (`research/market/voc-bank.md` / `.jsonl`): import its rows once — re-check each against [references/quote-bank.md](references/quote-bank.md), keep `id`s, drop vendor, agency, job-post and second-hand rows — and say so in Sources; the same for older market personas as candidate groups. Claims: only the read step of [`../../atoms/claims-gate/ATOM.md`](../../atoms/claims-gate/ATOM.md) now.
2b. **Since last time.** The Start steps of [`../../atoms/run-memory/ATOM.md`](../../atoms/run-memory/ATOM.md) for this run's job ([references/run-memory.md](references/run-memory.md): `audience research`, `prospect list`, `lead scoring` or a watch): the last record, what the ledger shows happened to earlier lists and alerts, whether last run's moves were done and what they led to — 3–5 lines that open the reply and the report, and that this run uses (weight what replied, don't repeat what didn't).
3. **Ask for the brand's own customer data — once, specifically, only when it has customers** (audience research only; in list mode it moves to the closing decisions) (paid plans live, reviews of the brand, users or orders mentioned in the brain) and no export is on file. One message, then wait:
   > "Before I start: your own customers are the best evidence, and I can't read them publicly. Could you share **<the export that fits the company type — [references/audience-research.md](references/audience-research.md) § Customer data>**, plus any **cancel reasons, win/loss notes or support themes**? Paste or upload it — or say "go" and I'll start from public sources."
   No customers yet (pre-launch), or the marketer says go: start the free pass. Personal data in an export never goes to the brain; only counts and patterns do.
4. **No other question before the report.** A list ask ("50 right-fit people with emails", a file to score, "tell me when…") goes **straight to list mode** — no audience pass first; it uses the saved buyer groups if they exist, else drafts the fit from the ask, the site and the brain, and offers the audience report afterwards. Out of scope (consumers as named individuals, journalists, investors, creators to pay): one line and the nearest thing this playbook does.

## Steps

1. **Frame — the brand's own side first.** If customer data came in, read it first ([references/audience-research.md](references/audience-research.md) § Customer data): who actually pays (company types, roles, plans, sizes), who stays and who leaves, the reasons they give. Then the brand's own site in the host browser (what it sells, to whom, pricing, logos, its words), the brain's `audience` text, and the latest market and competitor reports. Draft 2–5 candidate buyer groups by situation — never demographics — each with how to recognise one (usable as ad targeting or list criteria). Where the brand's own facts disagree (site vs brain vs customer data), customer data wins; otherwise make the conflict the first decision.

2. **News first.** Web search, last 90 days, for what is changing for these people: tools and platforms they must adopt, hiring and layoffs in their roles, budget shifts, regulation, the conversation everyone in the category is having.

3. **Research every group.** Pick the top three candidate groups for workers (a fourth or fifth only if the evidence demands it, with a light pass from you; its moves are labelled unverified unless they rest on a page you read). One worker per group — a subagent where the host supports it — given the text of [atoms/segment-pass](atoms/segment-pass/ATOM.md) as its whole brief, with the inputs it lists: wants, problems and how we solve them, first-hand words, where they gather, three moves, how rivals talk to them, inside its budget. Launch them together; never a worker per source or per topic. Without subagents, group by group, each written to `research/audience/segments/<slug>.md` before the next. Never pause for paid reads. **When they return, filter**: drop any quote from vendor, agency, trade-press, case-study or job-post text ([references/quote-bank.md](references/quote-bank.md)); pick the report's quotes so **at least 70% in every group are from the last 18 months** (older rows stay in the saved bank only); a group without enough recent first-hand quotes shows fewer and says "thin on recent voice".

4. **Size and roles.** Take the workers' counts; fill only the gaps yourself. For each group, a count of buyers or companies (bottom-up: directories, store or company counts, job-post counts, industry figures, the brand's own customer mix), each input sourced or labelled an assumption; community member counts are reach, never group size. Who uses, who pays, who approves.

5. **Synthesize — on the evidence you have.** Rank the groups on stated criteria (size, pain, fit, reach). Name the top-ranked group as the **lead**; if it doesn't clear the evidence bar ([references/audience-research.md](references/audience-research.md) § Evidence bar), label it "provisional — <what's missing>", keep it out of "In short" as a settled finding, and make the evidence gap a decision. **Every move rests on sources this run read**; a move on a channel it couldn't read is dropped or labelled "unverified — check the community and its rules first", and never becomes "Do this first". **For you** only where a finding matters ("nothing to do here" is fine). How rivals talk to them; don't follow the herd. Using the market and competitor reports read in step 2 (don't re-read them), say in one line where this one agrees or differs (a different lead group, a different first move).

6. **List mode (only when asked) — runs instead of steps 1–5** (step 2b still runs first, for the list's job). [references/list-mode.md](references/list-mode.md): build a list (sources picked by situation from [references/list-sources.md](references/list-sources.md)), score a list they bring ([references/score-a-list.md](references/score-a-list.md)), or set up a watch ([references/watches.md](references/watches.md)); with the outcomes ledger ([`../../atoms/outcomes-ledger/ATOM.md`](../../atoms/outcomes-ledger/ATOM.md), `entity: person`). Delivered as a short list report plus `list.csv`; a thin free result is said plainly, never padded. Uses the connector's people search and email checks when they exist ([references/data-sources.md](references/data-sources.md) § When the contact routes exist).

7. **Write and deliver.** Read [`../../atoms/write-report/ATOM.md`](../../atoms/write-report/ATOM.md) now and use the report shape in [references/audience-research.md](references/audience-research.md) § The report — its headings in its order. Write the report once; the brand copy is the same text. The sibling reports are the copies from step 2. Skip write-report's "offer to open it" — step 8 is the whole message. Through write-report (its length budget, banned-word check, decisions box, "what we're not doing" and source-skew line; "Do this first" names who does it; every group's unread sources merged into "What we couldn't check", each with what it would change), as a local file with a clickable link (an artifact or the full report in hosted chat). Save through [`../../atoms/save-to-brain/ATOM.md`](../../atoms/save-to-brain/ATOM.md): the report, the buyer groups and the quote bank (paths below), with a `gooseworks:file_url` link. Propose the buyer groups to the brand — skipped when the lead is provisional: **with `brain_*` tools**, one `gooseworks:brain_save` `audience` item per group (`logical_key` `research-audience.<group-slug>`, `who`, `pain`, `outcome`, `objections`, `triggers`, `channels`, a few short buyer `words` with no names, `is_primary` for the lead, `source: our_work`, `is_public: false`, `request_key` `research-audience.<YYYYMMDD>.<group-slug>`) — each waits for the marketer's yes in the app, and is never called confirmed; **otherwise** `gooseworks:brand_update` (`knowledge.audience`, `agent_proposal`, ≤ 2,000 characters, citing the bank). Then the End steps of run-memory: the run record ([references/run-memory.md](references/run-memory.md) § End), saved with save-to-brain.

8. **One closing message, this shape and nothing else** (write-report §2) — no file lists, no findings recap:
   > **Do this first:** <the move, who, by when>.
   > [<report title>](<local link>) · saved to your brand: [open](<file_url link>)
   > **Decisions:** <1–3 of: the evidence gap or brand-story choice · proof lines to sign off · "a paid read of <the forums we couldn't open> for up to N credits?" ([`../../atoms/spend-gate/ATOM.md`](../../atoms/spend-gate/ATOM.md) branch B) · a repeat (step 9)> · **want a list of named people in <lead group>?**
   The named-list offer is always the last decision unless a list was already asked for.

9. **Offer a repeat only when it helps** (AGENTS §8 Schedules: audience twice a year, or on a trigger — a new product, a new market, a big shift in who buys or why they leave). Say the reason, the cadence and the trigger for this brand. **Never repeat a list.** When accepted: created paused, per spend-gate §C, each run opening with what changed.

10. **On their answers.** Re-run only what changes, the top-up inside its ceiling (log every charge), re-deliver the same way. A rule the marketer states goes through [`../../atoms/save-learning/ATOM.md`](../../atoms/save-learning/ATOM.md) as `[outreach YYYY-MM-DD]`.

## Approval gates

- One upfront question is allowed — the customer-data ask, only when the brand has customers. Everything else free needs no yes. A paid top-up needs one yes to a ceiling; it stops there.
- Research recommends; it never makes the asset and never contacts anyone. In list mode the marketer sends from their own tool (D3); addresses are checked by the connector when it can, otherwise by the marketer before sending.
- The audience-field proposal is accepted by the owner in the app; schedules, saved rules and new claims only on the marketer's yes.

## Done when

"Since last time" opened the reply (or "no earlier run of this job"), and the run record was saved with counts and links only. The report passes write-report §3. Before it, at most one question — the customer-data ask, when the brand has customers. It names 2–5 buyer groups by situation, each sized in buyers or companies (sourced or labelled), with buying roles and how to recognise one; a lead group named, labelled provisional with what's missing when the evidence bar isn't met; for **every** group: what they want, at least three problems in their words with counts in a table marked solved / partly / not solved with proof status, first-hand quotes (at least five for the lead group, three for the others, or "thin" said plainly; at least 70% from the last 18 months), and three reach moves resting on sources this run read, each with who, what it needs, and a success check with a number and a date; at least four places the lead group gathers, with evidence the group is there. "In short" opens with "Do this first:" (move, who, why); the body is under 3,000 words; the closing message has the step 8 shape. A standard run stays inside the step 3 budget. Agreement or disagreement with the market and competitor reports is stated. The buyer groups and quote bank are saved. In list mode: the first rows arrive without an audience pass; every account passed its gates with a dated source; every person row has a dated title source, a contact route and an email status that says how far to trust it (verified only from a checker); every row has a specific fit reason and its signal (or "none"); region flags set; a shortfall stated with the universe size; the closing decisions offer the messages for the list. Score-a-list: the "answer first" leads named with why, every row with tier, confidence and action. A watch: created paused with a standing ceiling.
