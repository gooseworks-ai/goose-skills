# The list by company type

Load at list-mode step 1. Company type (from the brain's business context) sets the list unit, who counts as the buyer, the contact route that actually works and which signals matter. Pick the row; do not force a B2B SaaS shape onto a candle brand pitching boutiques.

| Seller | List unit | Who to find | Route that works | Signals that matter | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B2B SaaS** | Account × buying committee | Champion (owns the pain), economic buyer; user for PLG | Work email, LinkedIn | New leader, job posts naming the pain/tool, tech change, ex-champions at new companies, competitor customers (public case studies, review-site reviewers' companies) | Buying committee ≠ everyone in the department; respect the concentration cap |
| **B2B non-SaaS / industrial** | Distributor or OEM × territory | Purchasing manager, plant/ops manager, owner for small distributors | Phone and email; many contacts are not on LinkedIn | New facility, expansion, trade-show exhibitor lists, tenders, line-card changes | Exclude territories where the seller already has a distributor (channel conflict) — assume none excluded and put "which territories already have a distributor?" in the decisions |
| **DTC / consumer brand pitching retail or wholesale** (use the retail variant in fit-and-scoring.md; assume the chains/independents split and label it) | Retailer × category buyer | Category buyer / merchant; owner for independent shops | Buying-office inbox or vendor portal for chains (plus dated buyer rows); the shop's published inbox or IG for independents (UK sole traders: no email or cold DM) | Category review calendar, new store openings, competitors newly stocked, "local makers" programmes, trade-show exhibitor lists (never attendee or buyer lists) | Record door count and whether they stock direct competitors; chains often require a portal, not an email — say so |
| **CPG in retail** | Chain / distributor / broker × category | Category manager, broker principal | Email + broker introductions | Line reviews, regional expansion, distributor new-brand windows | Tier by door count; food service is a separate list |
| **Agency / services, or software sold to marketers** | Brand × marketing leader | Head of marketing/growth, founder at small brands | Work email, LinkedIn | Running ads now (ad libraries show volume and staleness), new marketing leader, hiring a creative or performance marketer, rebrand, launch | The ad-library view is the strongest free evidence — cite the ads. **For a low-price, self-serve tool, fit is a band, not "most ads wins":** about 5–60 live ads with creatives running more than 30 days (active, small enough to buy self-serve, and feeling the fatigue). Hundreds of live ads usually means an in-house team or an agency |
| **Marketplace (supply side)** | Provider × location/category | Owner or manager | IG, Google business listing, phone, contact form | Liquidity gaps the human names, new openings, review volume | Lists are larger (100–300+); fit means quality + location + gap, not company size |
| **Local business seeking B2B partners** | Business × radius | Owner or manager | Phone, walk-in, IG, contact form | New opening, complementary (non-competing) offer, local events | Keep to the radius the human sets; never list private individuals |
| **Developer tools / technical buyers** | Company × engineering buying group | Engineering or platform lead (buyer), staff engineer (champion) | Work email, LinkedIn — never commit emails or GitHub profiles as a route | Developer adoption on rivals' or category repos (company clusters), switch-intent posts on Hacker News and forums, rivals' public customers, conference talks, Product Hunt launches in the category | List companies from GitHub activity, never the developers; GitHub's terms forbid using its data for unsolicited email |
| **Consumer app** (partnerships, B2B2C) | Partner company × partnerships/BD lead | BD or partnerships lead | Work email, LinkedIn | Partner launching an integration programme, adjacent audience | Usually a small, hand-picked list (20–30) |
| **Nonprofit** | Organisation × CSR / foundation / partnerships lead | CSR, foundation programme officer, partnerships | Work email, published contact pages | Grant cycles, CSR reports, cause-aligned campaigns | Corporate and foundation contacts only — individual donors and wealth screening are out of scope |

If the seller does several (a DTC brand that also wants wholesale and corporate gifting), build one list per motion — different units, different routes, different segments.
