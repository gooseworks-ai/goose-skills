# List mode — named people to pitch (only when asked)

Load at step 5, only when the marketer asked for named people or accounts ("find me 50 right-fit people with emails", "an ABM target list", "retail buyers to pitch") or said yes to the offer in the closing message. The audience research runs first: the list is built on the primary segment (or the one named), and its "how to recognise one" becomes the fit definition.

## Order of work
1. **Fit definition** — [atoms/fit-definition](../atoms/fit-definition/ATOM.md) with [fit-and-scoring.md](fit-and-scoring.md) and [list-by-company-type.md](list-by-company-type.md): drafted from the segment, every line marked sourced or assumed.
2. **Accounts** — [atoms/account-sourcing](../atoms/account-sourcing/ATOM.md), free sources first.
3. **Timing** — [atoms/signals-and-hooks](../atoms/signals-and-hooks/ATOM.md) with [signals-and-hooks.md](signals-and-hooks.md), news first.
4. **People and routes** — [atoms/people-and-contacts](../atoms/people-and-contacts/ATOM.md) with [contacts-and-compliance.md](contacts-and-compliance.md); breadth first (names and titles for every kept account), then routes.
5. **Score, segment, QA** — [atoms/score-segment-qa](../atoms/score-segment-qa/ATOM.md).
6. **Ledger** — `../../../atoms/outcomes-ledger/ATOM.md`, `entity: person`: suppression loaded before sourcing, every row shown is ledgered, outcomes asked once in the closing message.

## In the report
A section "The list" after the segment it serves: the count found vs asked and why, what to send first, the angle (pain, proof, ask) in the segment's own words, before-you-send per region flag, and a link to `list.csv` (columns in [output-format.md](output-format.md)). The honest limit, once: there is no email checker here — addresses are published or pattern-backed and labelled; the marketer runs their own checker before sending. A thin free result is said plainly with the top-up that would fill it; never padded.
