# server-needs — research-audience

What this playbook needs that the GooseWorks MCP does not have (checked against the staging tool list on 2026-10-16). For each: the need, the step that needs it, and what the playbook does meanwhile. Ordered by priority.

| # | Need | Step | Meanwhile |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A free price check for public-data lookups** (the shared spend-gate's probe stands in) — `data_price_provider` covers fal and ElevenLabs only; `quote_only` exists only on `data_post_provider`. The server rule is "the customer's yes to the credit total first". | Gates 1–2 | The human sets a **ceiling in credits** at each gate; the playbook shows lookup counts by kind and the charges earlier answers actually reported, never its own multiplied total, and stops before the next lookup could pass the ceiling. On a first-ever run, one lookup is spent before any price is known. |
| 2 | **Email verification** (valid / catch-all / risky / invalid) through the proxy | 6, 8 | Addresses are `published` or `pattern-guess`, never `verified`. The human runs their own verifier and pastes results back; the playbook merges them. The spec's "verified emails" is met only through the human until this exists. |
| 3 | **People and company search** (title, function, seniority, company size, industry, geography) — a B2B contact database through the proxy | 4, 6 | Web search, company pages and public profiles. Works, but costs roughly 13–20 lookups per delivered person, and many people are not public. |
| 4 | **Work-email finder** for a named person at a domain | 6 | Published addresses, or pattern-guesses backed by a published address at the same domain. Many rows end up LinkedIn-only. |
| 5 | **Web page reading through the proxy** (a company's team, careers, contact and press pages) for hosts with no web tool — hosted chat connectors have no shell | 4–6 | Uses the host's own web reading when present; otherwise search results and company pages only, and `published` emails are rare — the summary says so. |
| 6 | **A lists / contacts store** in the product: list rows, per-person status (listed / contacted / replied / meeting / bounced / unsubscribed), suppression every playbook respects, visible in the app | 8, outcomes | Files in the brand coworker workspace, `research/audience/` (the §8 convention). Risk: a brand with no coworker falls back to the caller's own workspace, where a teammate would not see the ledger and could re-list suppressed people. Personal data stays out of the brand brain on purpose. |
| 7 | **Scheduled runs with an enforced credit ceiling** — `automation_upsert` agent jobs run the coworker (which uses credits) and have no spend-limit field, so the per-run ceiling lives only in the job's prompt | 9 | Per the shared spend-gate automation rule: offered opt-in, created paused, the human's yes names a standing per-run data ceiling and that the run itself uses credits; never data spend in a scheduled run without one. Each list also carries a `recheck_by` date flagged free on the next run. |
| 8 | **Hiring and job-change feeds** (job posts by company and function; role changes for named people) | 5 | Job posts via web search on job boards and careers pages; job changes only from public profiles of people the human named. "Champion moved" / "started hiring" watchers cannot run cheaply. |
| 9 | **Fresh firmographics and funding** — the company-page funding block can be years stale | 5 | Funding counts only with a dated press source, and never alone. |
| 10 | **A structured outbound-ICP field** in the brand kit, separate from the consumer `audience` text | 1, write-back | Saved as a dated brand document; the consumer `audience` field is never written here (research-market owns it). |
| 11 | **CRM read** (customers, open deals, past contacts) for exclusions | Before you start | The human uploads or pastes an export; asked, never required. |
| 12 | **Document import cost stated in the tool** — `knowledge_import` says a source "may spend credits" | write-back | Verified free on staging for uploaded documents (research session, 2026-10-16); the tool description should say so. |
| 13 | **A sending layer** (sequences, domains, warm-up, reply handling) | — | Out of scope; do-outreach (backlog) waits on it. The human sends from their own tool (D3). |
